Interpretation

The vessel conserves modeled biomass, not energy. The green trend now shows edible shoots—not total plant reserves—because roots and seeds are unavailable to grazers. The lines still use separate vertical scales and are not a mass-balance plot; use the ledger and the thin mass-partition strip for accounting.

What this model actually claims

This is a transparent individual-based trophic model, not a calibrated reconstruction of a particular ecosystem. Its rigor comes from explicit assumptions, a fixed integration step, local sensing, heritable traits, and exact transfer accounting.

Closed biomass, open energy

  • Plants transfer mineral nutrient into edible shoots and bounded root reserves, powered by external sunlight.
  • Defoliated plants can remobilize roots into shoots. Roots turn over into litter, and dormant seeds slowly die back into nutrient, so protected plant compartments cannot become permanent mass sinks.
  • Mature plants transfer some shoot mass into an explicit seed bank only when recruitment space and seed-bank capacity exist.
  • Unassimilated food and corpses become detritus. Respiration and decomposition return mass to nutrient.
  • Birth splits parental body mass. No plant, prey, or predator is created for free.

Evolution

Prey inherit speed, sensing range, fear, and metabolic economy. Predators inherit speed, sensing range, pursuit drive, and metabolic economy. Mutations are bounded multiplicative perturbations. Faster motion improves encounters or escape but increases locomotion cost. Predation uses handling time and a low-density refuge response. Sparse prey also experience less effective panic pressure because they are assumed to occupy cover; this prevents a few starving predators from keeping the final prey in permanent flight. Both trophic levels reproduce from sustained body condition rather than one lucky meal.

Controls

Choose a tool and tap the arena. New organisms are assembled from the existing nutrient pool; recycling returns all of their modeled biomass. The thin strip at the bottom of the trend chart shows the current mass partition. At 4×, one real second advances roughly four model days.
