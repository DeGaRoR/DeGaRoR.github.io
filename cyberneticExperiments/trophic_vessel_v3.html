Interpretation

The vessel conserves modeled biomass, not energy. The trend lines use separate vertical scales and therefore are not a mass-balance plot. The ledger includes dissolved nutrient, edible shoots, protected roots, dormant seeds, consumers, and detritus.

What this model actually claims

This is a transparent individual-based trophic model, not a calibrated reconstruction of a particular ecosystem. Its rigor comes from explicit assumptions, a fixed integration step, local sensing, heritable traits, and exact transfer accounting.

Closed biomass, open energy

  • Plants transfer nutrient into shoots and protected root reserves, powered by external sunlight.
  • Mature plants transfer some shoot mass into an explicit dormant seed bank; sparse vegetation recruits faster from that bank.
  • Unassimilated food and corpses become detritus. Respiration and decomposition return mass to nutrient.
  • Birth splits parental body mass. No plant, prey, or predator is created for free.

Evolution

Prey inherit speed, sensing range, fear, and metabolic economy. Predators inherit speed, sensing range, pursuit drive, and metabolic economy. Mutations are bounded multiplicative perturbations. Faster motion improves encounters or escape but increases locomotion cost. Predation uses handling time and a low-density refuge response, so predators become inefficient when prey are rare instead of deterministically erasing the last survivors. Hunger raises pursuit effort and capture probability, while well-fed predators rest and accumulate reproductive readiness; assimilation itself remains constant.

Controls

Choose a tool and tap the arena. New organisms are assembled from the existing nutrient pool; recycling returns all of their modeled biomass. The thin strip at the bottom of the trend chart shows the current mass partition. At 4×, one real second advances roughly four model days.
