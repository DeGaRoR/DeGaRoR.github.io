# Vegetation placement maps over Mount Ardent, from ROCK-STUDY (16 Nov 2026), for the subalpine-biome session

These maps cover 2 km x 2 km of Jolene round Mount Ardent (17 Nov: was 1.6 km). They are a by-product of the rock study's macro bake
(`flyDiy/tools/rock_study/trond_gen.py`, `macro()`), computed from the island's own data:

- the 5 m IFSAR DEM's height, slope and 20 m curvature;
- a D8 flow accumulation;
- the 10 m land-cover raster's rock share;
- the rock study's joint network.

To regenerate them:

```bash
node flyDiy/tools/rock_study/export_patch.js
```

```bash
python3 flyDiy/tools/rock_study/trond_gen.py --only macro
```

**Frame.** `veg_maps.json` holds the frame:
- 1024 x 1024 pixels at 1.953 m each;
- rows run along z (south), columns along x (east);
- pixel (0, 0) is world (9540 - 1000, -5140 - 1000) = (8540, -6140);
- world x = 8540 + (col + 0.5) x 1.953, world z = -6140 + (row + 0.5) x 1.953.

The full-resolution 2560² maps (0.78 m a pixel) are in `flyDiy/tools/rock_study/data/trond/`, which is gitignored and
regenerated by the two commands above.

`veg_maps_1024.png` is RGBA, each channel 0..255:

| channel | meaning | use |
|---|---|---|
| R | rock exposure: rock on the high, convex, steep ground; vegetation down the drainage and in hollows; a 2-40 m mosaic | 0 = no bare rock |
| G | vegetation suitability: the joints and veins, the hollows of the rock's masses, low-angle benches, the outcrops' margins, everything not rock | the density of small plants |
| B | wetness: flow accumulation + concavity + flatness | sedge and muskeg high, krummholz low |
| A | soil depth proxy: not rock, concave, gentle | shrubs need a little, trees more |

`veg_class_1024.png` is 8-bit, one class per pixel:

| class | meaning | share of the extent | colour in `veg_class_preview.png` |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | bare rock | 22 % | grey |
| 1 | crack and vein flora (moss, grass, ferns in the joints) | 15 % | olive |
| 2 | heath, dwarf shrubs (shallow soil on rock) | 19 % | tan |
| 3 | krummholz, subalpine conifers (deeper pockets, sheltered, not wet) | 24 % | mid green |
| 4 | wet sedge / muskeg (flat, water gathering) | 0.3 % | blue-grey |
| 5 | forest (under ~420 m, deep soil) | 19 % | dark green |

**Caveats:**
- Thresholds are first guesses, readable in `trond_gen.py` (search `THE VEGETATION MAPS`).
- Muskeg is nearly absent here because this patch is a mountain. The game's own raster carries muskeg on the flats.
- The maps follow the rock study's exposure, which is designed against the user's Google Maps reference of Annette:
  rock on the tops and ridges, vegetation cutting through everywhere.
