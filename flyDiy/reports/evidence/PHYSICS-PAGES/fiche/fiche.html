What the fiche is (draft)

The fiche is how a player looks at an aeroplane without flying it: a set of printable cream sheets, one subject each, in a fixed order. Every page is drawn from the game's own data (its renders and the numbers the simulation flies with), never typed in by hand. Each prints and shares as one 3:2 sheet.

From the plane list: two buttons

LoadLook

Load puts the aeroplane on the stand. Look opens its fiche, without touching what is on the stand, and could later hold a turnable 3D view. Every baked list plane (24 today) has its fiche ready at once.

To settle before it is one piece

  • The order (decided 12 Nov): Main, Cutaway, Flight log, then Mass, Drag, Physics, Electrics, Integrity, Reference. One line in AIRCRAFT-LOOKS' page strip.
  • One sheet per page. Every page is one 3:2 cream sheet except Mass, which today is a long scrolling page without the sheet's frame (see its capture). Folding it into one sheet, as the prototype was, makes it print and share like the rest.
  • Cream only (decided 12 Nov). The dark variant of the physics pages is dropped; every page is the framed 3:2 cream sheet.
  • A self-built plane. Its fiche is drawn at its certificate. Its physics pages are worked out while it stands in the garage. It has no Reference page.
