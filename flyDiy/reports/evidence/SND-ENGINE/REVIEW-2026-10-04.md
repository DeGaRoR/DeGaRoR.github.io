## What it says

1. **The running voice is right.** Every sweep and run-up (7/7) is Right: the port, the firing order, the pipes, the load
   law and the jitter are accepted. Do not retune them.
2. **THE STARTER is the defect** (4 of 5 starts Close/Wrong): the DC whine is too high-pitched, too synthetic, too
   present ("R2D2"); the engine under it too weak during cranking and the catch. A real starter is a low, gritty,
   geared growl dominated by the cranking engine's compression strokes, not a tonal whine.
3. **THE AFTER-SHUTDOWN TICKING is rejected**: modal ringing reads as bells. Real cooling ticks are dry, short, broadband
   clicks with almost no ring (thermal contraction of the exhaust and heat shields), irregular, low level.
4. **THE CLICKS** (misfire pops, exhaust coughs, the 582's combustion transients) are too loud against the engine and
   not blended: lower, softer (shorter rise, low-passed), and inside the engine's body rather than on top of it.
5. **LOUDNESS**: the twin 582 summed is too loud and tiring — per-voice level when several engines run, and the
   two-stroke's level in the level law.
6. The O-540's start: its low growl a little lower in pitch; its high layer (likely the starter whine) too synthetic.
