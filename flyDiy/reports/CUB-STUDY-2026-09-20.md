## 3. The traps

- **The stab and fin layers overwrote the stock cub's tail on every load**
  (`_cage_stab.js` / `_cage_fin.js`: `Object.assign(PAGE.presets['piper
  cub'], stOwn / FIN_PARAMS+FIN_CUB)` — "so the preset is self-contained
  against the page's jodel tail"): `stSpan` ← `finHeight` = 1, and the row's
  1.41 never reached the shelf or the card. G445.4: fill, never overwrite —
  a key the row does not state still takes the Cub's value.
- `CAGE_ENG_APPLY_PRESET` writes the preset index in ITS list ('bare engine'
  at 0), the join reads `engPreset` in the layer's (the A-65 at 0): applied
  headless, the A-65 landed as index 1 → the audit compared A-65 dials with
  another preset → "custom engine". The re-join script pins the index.
- The 45 L nose tank reads "through the crew (48 points)" on the bench (the
  21 L one did not): the nose bay's rule re-stations any `along` outside the
  field-clamped bay, and a wider or taller box "runs out of the body". Left
  at the rule's spot; an ENERGY item (a nose tank ABOVE the pilot's legs, as
  the J-3's is, needs the bay's height band, not a station).
