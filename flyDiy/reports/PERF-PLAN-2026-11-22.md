## The order

Step 1, brute force:
1. Why a second load is not faster than the first (PERF-LEAD). Shader text between boots: ruled out (726 of 727 texts
   identical, `tools/perf/src_diff.js`). Left: Chrome's 6 MiB GPU program cache; the benchmark's "warm" was a same-tab
   reload after a whole session. Rig: `tools/perf/warm_cache_test.sh`.
2. The engine explains itself (PERF-TRACE): every program owned, the loading timeline, the freeze ledger.
3. Compiling that never blocks a frame, the garage's programs first (PERF-LEAD). Includes three's compileAsync "ready"
   continuation doing first-use work in one task (123 ms in flight).
4. The world build (PERF-WORLDBUILD): 39 s, all on the main thread. Airstrips, premises patch and roads go to the house
   worker (617 MB of geometry: no baked files). THE RULE: the reveal waits for the near set only (3 km); everything else
   is built nearest first in the worker and arrives after. About 25 s of builders today, about 4 s needed before the
   reveal (node figures). Opt-in (`?stripw=1`, `?nearfirst=1`) until the user has seen the reveal before/after in game.
   The settle step is a fixed 1 500 updates, not a readiness test: an opt-in flag, default unchanged.
5. Fewer shader variants (PERF-LEAD): one light count day and night (164 programs added at noon today), identical
   programs merged. Aimed at the stall at a change of time of day; cold-load seconds possible, not promised.
6. The heavy programs (PERF-LEAD, after the captures): pavement ~27 s alone, ground 7-33 s; their maths into baked
   textures (Codex's prototypes), if the captures say HEAVY-BOUND.

Step 2, architecture:
7. Garage first (PERF-LEAD): the plain studio in under 20 s, the hangar fading in, the world building and compiling
   behind it, Go in 20 s at most. Built on what exists: the garage-only boot, the studio, the boot's step list with
   declared prerequisites. Main risk of the plan: background loading that makes the garage stutter.
8. Live loading in the world (PERF-LEAD): detail arriving over the first 20 s under a frame budget.

Later: the coordinator (one shared per-frame budget, piloted on one airport approach) once the freeze ledger exists.

## Rules of the work

- A gain counts when a whole cold load shows it, same build on both sides, more than once, with the CPU package
  temperature logged (the box throttles under sustained load).
- Nothing visible changes without a before/after in game, on a page, for the user.
- Each step is its own ready with its numbers against the official table. A0 keeps trains, the box and the cold loads.
- Program owners are metadata (`PROGRAMS.own(material, 'feature / system / variant')` or `userData.progOwner`), never
  the cache key, never the GLSL. GLSL, defines and variants are PERF-LEAD's.
- A builder that runs behind the garage is in a worker or a resumable step under ~8 ms of main thread a slice.
