study notes

  • why a shear, not a rotation — the pillar's ring pair keeps its section (x, y untouched); only z slides as a function of height. A rotation would shrink the section's projection and pinch the band.
  • why no bump is expected — the surface is GENERATED from the rings (bays are single Catmull-Clark spans), so tilting a ring re-slopes the spans on either side; there is no pre-existing surface to dent. The fold probe below the stat line measures the sharpest smooth-edge crease near the pillar, leaned vs 0°.
  • the clamp — the failure mode is displacement, not angle: roof-level travel is held to half the shorter neighbouring bay (the readout shows the effective angle when it engages). The lean lives in cageResolve, so interior, zones and window frames all read the tilted rings. See the study file in futureDesigns/.
