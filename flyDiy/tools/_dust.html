The dust follows the soil: dry is full clouds, damp is a thin skirt, wet is mud clods and no dust. In the game the wetness comes from the last four days of the weather plan.

Each puff grows and thins on its own (turbulent diffusion), drifts with the wind, and loses its coarse grains in seconds while the fines hang for a minute or more. The sun scatters forward through it, so dust with the sun behind it glows.

On the S20, please note the fps chip during a take-off on "Phone". That number decides the phone budget.
