# TOWN KIT: THE ARCHETYPES AND THE PACK (G850, 2026-09-27)

QUEUE-C C3a. Source: ARCH-2026-09-27 §4 (towns as an instanced kit). Node only. **Nothing in the game changes**:
Metlakatla stays off (`?town=1`, G590). C3b builds the renderer after your look review.

**The contact sheet: [`TOWNKIT-2026-09-27.png`](TOWNKIT-2026-09-27.png)** (272 KB). It shows all 33 archetypes at
lod 0, at one scale so S/M/L read as sizes, each in the paint its source house was sampled with. It is flat-shaded
by a small software rasteriser in node, so the textures, dirt and sag are missing and it is not the game's look.
It is there to judge the SHAPES.

## What was made

- **Sown exactly as the game sows it:** Jolene's record (`tools/fixtures/island_jolene.json`) composed by
  `PG.compose`, then for each plot `render_premises.js buildHouse`'s own recipe up to the house's P. That is
  `VILLAGE_GEN.placeHouse` with the same seeds, rules and picks. It gives **459 plots** (Metlakatla 375, the
  village 84) and **63 outbuildings** (from `finishPlot`).
- **Clustered:** the houses are first split by what cannot be faked: land 437, water 22, storefront 11 (a
  storefront's window, awning and false front are its whole face). Inside each split, k-medoids runs on a "how
  different it looks" distance: roof family (hip counted as its own) > storeys > stance > footprint / pitch /
  storey height > porch, lean-to, dormers, bay, log corners.
  **Each archetype is a medoid, i.e. a house the sampler really drew, never an average.** There are 30 house
  archetypes (28 land, 1 water, 1 storefront) and the 3 outbuilding presets (storage shed, outhouse, garage).
- **Built:** each medoid's P is made canonical: flat ground, and the floor at the cluster's median clearance over
  the high corner. The per-lot dressing is left out. Those pieces stay with the lot or are already instanced
  props: the pier, the yard, the people, the woodpile, the barrel, the lamps, the smoke and the ground skirt.
  Each archetype is built by HOUSE_GEN at **lod 0 and lod 1** (the generator's own two meshes), plus **the G594
  box**.
- **Every vertex carries:**
  - its **ROLE**: its bag in `HOUSE_GEN.BAGS`. The finish is no longer baked in; C3b's TARR slot becomes role ×
    the instance's finish set.
  - a **STANCE** weight: 1 on the ground, 0 from the floor structure up, and a straight line in between. The
    posts, piles, cripple walls, stairs and downpipes then stretch down to the instance's own ground in the
    vertex shader.
- Every pane carries its glow index. The instance's lights byte says how much of the house is lit.

- **Every plot has a fitting archetype.** The fit is checked on the decoded 32 B record alone:
  - the whole plan inside the plot, the porch and stairs included;
  - water only on the water;
  - no slab on more than 0.55 m of fall;
  - the stretch within ±10 %;
  - the four ground offsets matching the terrain under the corners.

  One garage needed a 0.5 m nudge. 443 of the 459 houses got the archetype of their own cluster; the other 16 got a
  neighbour, because their own did not fit the plot.
- **Repetition, Metlakatla:** the 375 houses use all 30 archetypes, at most 24 per archetype (median 12).
  - Shapes: 269 of the 522 instances are mirrored, which gives 65 different shape + mirror pairs.
  - Looks: 48 finish sets and the sampled paint give **341 different looks** (archetype × mirror × finish × wall
    and roof paint) before any per-instance dirt or weather.
  - Stretch: 173 houses want more stretch than the ±10 % allowed and are held at it.

## The formats (all in `tools/town_kit.js`)

- **The pack** is one gzip stream, named by its sha256, in this order: a 12 B preamble, a JSON header, then the
  blobs. The header holds:
  - `bags`, i.e. the role table;
  - the finish sets;
  - per archetype: its source, its shape keys, its floor and the stance top, its footprint, its roof kind, and
    per LOD the counts, the quantization boxes and the blob offsets.

  Each vertex is **16 B**:

  | bytes | field |
  |---|---|
  | 0-5 | u16 × 3 position, in the LOD's own box |
  | 6-7 | i8 × 2 octahedral normal |
  | 8-11 | u16 × 2 UV, in metres, in the LOD's own UV range |
  | 12 | u8 AO |
  | 13 | u8 role |
  | 14 | u8 stance |
  | 15 | u8 glow |

  Glass and pane vertices also carry a 4 B side stream: the pane's width and height in 2 cm, and its dressing code
  (for the curtains).
- **The instance table** is 32 B a house:

  | bytes | field |
  |---|---|
  | 0-11 | f32 x, z, y (the premises frame; y is the ground under the high corner) |
  | 12-13 | u16 yaw |
  | 14 | u8 archetype (bit 7: mirrored) |
  | 15 | u8 finish set |
  | 16-18 | u8 wall, trim and roof paint |
  | 19-21 | u8 weather, dirt, lights |
  | 22 | i8 stretch along the ridge |
  | 23 | u8 flags (outbuilding, water plot, named preset) |
  | 24-31 | i16 × 4 ground offsets at the footprint's corners, cm |
- **The roof kind is geometry, not paint.** Standing seams (galv, rust) and a ridge cap (any metal set) are built.
  So an instance may only wear a finish set whose roof is of its archetype's kind; GATE TOWNKIT holds this.

## Decisions for you (and C3b)

1. **Commit the pack to `media/`?** `node tools/town_kit.js --media` writes 4.5 MB into `media/townkit/` and its
   manifest `src/core/townkit_pack.json`; GATE MEDIA and GATE TOWNKIT already know both. It is NOT done: git keeps
   every bake forever, and any change to `_house_gen.js` makes a new pack. GATE TOWNKIT reports a stale pack but
   does not fail on it. Proposal: bake it once C3b's renderer needs it, and again only when the kit ships.
2. **The waterfront** is one archetype for 22 plots, and it stands on the ground stretch: the stair reaches the
   seabed rather than a landing, because the pier and the landing are the lot's. If the look review cares about
   the harbour, give it two or three archetypes (`--k` or a floor per split) and let C3b draw the jetty with the
   pier.
3. **The count.** 30 was ARCH's figure, and the sheet shows how much 30 covers. Measured: `--k 20` gives a
   2.95 MB gz pack, 30 gives 4.52 MB and 40 gives 5.88 MB. Every plot still fits at all three counts.

## Found on the way (not fixed here)

- **render_premises.js `buildHouse` reads `world.waterH(0, 0)` for the tide.** On Jolene that answers for the
  world origin, which is dry land, and returns `-Infinity`. So every waterfront house the game sows:
  - is placed with an infinite tide, its centre walked out to the plot's depth limit;
  - gets no landing, jetty or pier.

  Measured in node on four `z_harbour` plots. The sea is 0 (GATE PREMISES 14's own note), and the kit sows with 0.
  This is C3c's to fix when Metlakatla returns.
- **The game plans the outbuildings in streaming order,** with one fence set for the whole premises. Which edges a
  lot fences, and so the random numbers its outbuilding draws afterwards, depends on that order. The kit plans in
  plot order, so C2b's cook should fix one order for both.
