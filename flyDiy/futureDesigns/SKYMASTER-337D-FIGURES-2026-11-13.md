# Cessna 337D Super Skymaster (1969): figures for the reproduction (A0, 13 Nov 2026)

Source: the 1969 Cessna 337D Owner's Manual (the user's copy; facts extracted, page refs to the manual's sections) + the manufacturer's "Principal Dimensions" 3-view. The user has the PDF and the 3-view; ask A0 or the user to attach them in your chat if you need the curves (loading graph, CG moment envelope, performance tables).

## Dimensions (3-view)
- Span 38 ft 0 in (11.58 m); length 29 ft 10 in (9.09 m); height 9 ft 4 in max (2.84 m).
- Boom centreline spacing: 10 ft 0 3/8 in at the tail (3.06 m), 10 ft 8 1/4 in at the wing (3.26 m).
- Propellers: 76 in (1.93 m) diameter, front AND rear; constant speed, full feathering.
- Main gear track 8 ft 2 in (2.49 m); ground attitude 2 deg nose up (side view).
- Layout: high wing, pod fuselage with a tractor engine in the nose and a pusher engine at the rear of the pod (centreline thrust, push-pull); twin booms from the wing to a horizontal stabiliser + elevator between the two fins; tricycle gear, retractable (mains swing aft into the pod sides / booms; nose aft).

## Engines and fuel (Section I / specifications)
- 2 x Continental IO-360-C, fuel injected, 210 BHP at 2800 RPM each.
- Fuel: 93 gal total standard (two main tanks of 46 gal in the wings; sump tanks in the lower part of each boom), 131 gal with optional auxiliary tanks. Oil 5 gal per engine (spec table).
- Cowl flaps (electric) front and rear.

## Weights (specifications)
- Gross weight 4400 lb (1996 kg). Empty weight approx. 2655 lb (1204 kg). Baggage 365 lb.
- Wing loading 21.9 lb/sq ft -> wing area ~201 sq ft (18.7 m2). Power loading 10.5 lb/hp.
- CG limits: from the manual's "Center of Gravity Moment Envelope" chart (Section IV); not in the text extract: get them from the PDF.

## Performance (specifications, 4400 lb column)
- Top speed at sea level 199 mph; cruise 75 % at 5500 ft 191 mph; range 760 mi (92 gal, 75 %, no reserve), optimum range at 10 000 ft 144 mph / 1345 mi.
- Rate of climb at sea level: twin 1200 fpm; front engine only 300 fpm; rear engine only 385 fpm (the rear engine is the better one alone).
- Service ceiling: twin 19 500 ft; front only 6800 ft; rear only 8800 ft.
- Take-off: ground run 895 ft; over 50 ft 1545 ft. Landing: roll 700 ft; over 50 ft 1650 ft.
- Stall speed flaps down, power off: 69 mph.
- Best rate / best angle of climb: flaps up gear up 113 / 82 mph; flaps 1/3 gear down 97 / 79 mph; flaps full gear down 84 / 73 mph. Normal climb 115 mph; climb 113 mph SL -> 107 mph at 10 000 ft. Best single-engine rate of climb 100 mph IAS (blue line).
- Approach 90-100 mph flaps extended; normal take-off with flaps 1/3.

## Limitations (Section IV, CAS)
- Normal category. Flight load factor: flaps up +3.8 / -1.52 g; flaps down +2.0 g (design loads 150 % of these).
- Never exceed 225 mph; max structural cruising 190 mph; flaps 1/3 160 mph; flaps 1/3 to full 120 mph; gear operating 140 mph (gear down below 140); gear extended 155 mph.
- Markings: green arc 79-190 mph; white arc (flaps 1/3 to full) 70-120 mph; yellow 190-225.
- Engines 2800 RPM red line.
- Wing flap - elevator trim interconnect (mechanical): extending flaps rotates the elevator trim nose-down automatically.

## What it tests in the game (coverage, A0 v2)
Twin booms, a stabiliser between the booms, push-pull centreline thrust, a new retract fold, exterior lights; engine-out behaviour with NO asymmetric yaw (centreline thrust: the front-out vs rear-out climb numbers above are the check).
