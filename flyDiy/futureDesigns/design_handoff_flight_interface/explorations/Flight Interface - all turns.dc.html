1The flight interface, rebaselined — one system with the editor

Read from the built code, not from memory: body.html (the #ui layer, verbatim ids and copy), style.css (the amber-on-glass palette, still IBM Plex Mono), editor.css + editor.js (the Bone palette, #edRail/#edFly, the plate metrics, the real icon paths — reused here), app.js (the 14 phases, the PFD, the telemetry cells, the arrival card, the cockpit eye), bench.js and the ROADMAP's floating chantiers (F4 day cycle, F5 atmosphere, F6 aerodrome) for what has to fit later.

The rule, and every decision below follows from it. The editor's rule is spatial — every surface has exactly one job. Flight has a before, during and after that the editor does not, so its rule is temporal: the screen answers three questions in the order the flight asks them — what am I flying · what is it doing · what happened — and nothing that answers one stays on screen while another is being asked. The floating-plate grammar is the editor's, unchanged: plates over the render, top = what you do with it, bottom = how you look at it, one flyout per question.
