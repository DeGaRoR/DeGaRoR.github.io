# PHOTO MODE 2: one photo bar, the high-quality shot, saved scenes, and a teleport

Design note, 11 Nov 2026, PHOTO-MODE-2 (cloud session). Written as the landing week's design; the user then said
"can you start now?", so P0 is built (below). The Steam page (5-10 screenshots, mid-December) will use the tool, but
making those scenes is NOT this scope; making the tool usable by any agent or session on its own IS (`tools/PHOTO.md`).

**Where the user landed (11 Nov evening, section 6):**
- the bar is the flight interface's own TOP BAR (all the options there), the shutter a floating half-transparent
  round button (1.2);
- the picture size is a choice, the window's by default (2.5);
- HIGH QUALITY is OPT-IN, with the night hero picture's settings (ultra, density 200, full trees to 150 m, real trees
  to 1000 m) scaled to the player's machine (2.2b); it may take longer than 10 s with a spinner in the middle that keeps
  you company, HARD-CAPPED at 55 s (`SCENE_HQ_CAP_S`), and the browser must never think the page froze (2.3);
- no posing tools: save the scene where you are, load it back, and a "go to" the start places (4);
- one shutter.

**Status (12 Nov, evening): P0-P5 BUILT** on branch claude/optimistic-dirac-1zu6u1 (the user: "progress until you have
a finished product"). What is left is TUNING ON A GPU (P4's frame counts and the tree wait with real trees to 1 km, a 4K
picture's memory) and two drawn details a scene does not set yet (the control surfaces / prop, the camera's roll). The
how-to, kept current: `tools/PHOTO.md`.

Companion files: `futureDesigns/photo-mode-2/scene.schema.json` (the scene format, JSON Schema 2020-12) and
`futureDesigns/photo-mode-2/example_cub_lagoon.scene.json` (one scene, filled in).

The user's four asks (11 Nov):
1. ONE photo mode: the same bar in the garage and in flight.
2. A Gran Turismo-quality shot. At the shutter, ONE frame is re-rendered at the maximum (trees to the horizon,
   reflective water, the best shadows, about 2x resolution, full mist and clouds), then every setting goes back.
   Up to 5-10 s a picture is fine, but the interface NEVER freezes, and a clear status shows progress.
3. SAVED SCENES. A scene holds the camera, the time and weather, the plane, its pose and the quality. It is saved,
   exported and imported as a small file, and re-opened EXACTLY. The same files drive Claude's headless picture rigs.
4. The plane can be posed anywhere (later, the user: no posing tools; save where you are, load it, a teleport - section 4).

## 1. The unified photo bar

### 1.1 One module

The bar leaves app.js for its own file, `src/viewer/photo.js` (`window.PHOTO`), in the RENDER slot before app.js. app.js
keeps only the hooks it already has: `photoHook` from `shotSet`, and `photoGrab` at the frame's end. app.js gives the
module an api object, as it does for `editorInit`, so photo.js never reaches into app.js's closure:

```js
PHOTO.init({
  site: () => inGarage ? 'garage' : 'flight',
  hold: on => gmHold(on), running: () => running,
  camera: { get, set, rows(body) },   // garage: orbit + garageFov; flight: FL_BUILD.camera + devCam
  grab: fn => { photoGrab = fn; },     // the next presented frame's canvas, as today
  pose: {...}, scene: {...}             // sections 4 and 3
});
```

`photoBegin`'s `if (inGarage) return` goes away. The garage's 'screenshot' pill (editor.js:3086) and the flight's
corner camera button both call `SHOT_MODE.enter()`, and `photoHook` opens the same bar in both places.

### 1.2 What the bar holds: the flight interface's own top bar (the user, 11 Nov evening)

The bar follows `futureDesigns/design_handoff_flight_interface/README.md` (turn 2, the one being implemented), not a
panel of its own: "all the options are on the top bar". In photo mode the flight screen keeps its grammar and changes
what it holds:

- **The top bar** (`left:22px; right:22px; top:18px`, the editor's `#edTopBar` geometry): at the LEFT the plate,
  `PHOTO` + its meta (`paused · <scene name>`), with five slots, each opening its flyout UNDER its own slot
  (`openFly`'s rule); at the RIGHT, the verb `Done`. The same bar in the garage (its top bar is the same grammar).
- **The rest of the chrome leaves**: the look rail (bottom left), the PFD (bottom right), the map and the trace. The
  picture is the screen.
- **The shutter**: a round button floating over the picture, bottom centre, half transparent, no plate and no frame
  around it (the phone camera's shutter). The only thing below the top bar. It hides itself for the picture (the
  picture is the 3D frame alone, as today).
- Palette and type: the Bone tokens (`--ed-plate`, `--ed-ink`, `--ed-acc` ...), IBM Plex Sans, 9 px plate radius,
  `backdrop-filter: blur(14px)`. Nothing new.

| slot | its flyout holds | garage | flight |
|---|---|---|---|
| **camera** | the views, free, FOV, level, lead (`FL_BUILD.camera`'s rows); free: WASD / ZQSD, R / F, Shift, drag | orbit / free, FOV 25-80 | as today's photo bar |
| **light** | time of day (slider + the 8 presets), date, clouds, wind; the clock frozen while in photo mode | the shed's mood | hidden in the career (its clock) |
| **picture** | size: window (default), 1920x1080, 2560x1440, 3840x2160, custom; **High quality** OFF by default (opt-in, remembered), with the machine's note (2.2b) | same | same |
| **scene** | Save this scene · Load a scene (the list: name, thumbnail, date) · Export · Import (a `.scene.json` or a picture) | same | same |
| **go to** | the island's aerodromes and strips: pick one, the aeroplane is put on its stand (section 4) | - | flight only |

Rules:
- Photo mode is a BUBBLE. On Done, everything it changed goes back: camera mode and FOV (as today), the clock and
  its rate, the weather, the pause state. A scene loaded or a "go to" is the exception: that is a place you chose, and
  you stay there.
- The shutter is always instant feedback: the flash and the sound fire at the press, inside the gesture (G3744 as
  today), even when the high-quality shot then takes 20 s.
- Esc closes the bar (as today). During a high-quality shot, Esc and the spinner's ✕ cancel it, and the restore runs.

### 1.3 Cost and risk

- Cost: about 1 session. It is mostly moving code (`app.js:19564-19623` out to photo.js), plus the garage's camera rows.
- Risk: low. GATE UISMOKE (+ `--phone`) runs every button handler in a vm. Add rows: the photo top bar in the garage
  and in flight, the shutter, the phone, and Done restoring the camera and pause. GATE UILAYER U1/U2 already holds
  "photo mode does not see the helpers".

## 2. The high-quality shot (the Gran Turismo pipeline)

### 2.1 The idea

The picture is the game's own frame, from the same `aa.render` (`app.js:20764`) and the same `photoGrab` hook. For
a few seconds the world is pushed to settings no preset uses, and the frame is not grabbed until every part reports
ready. It is a small state machine stepped once per `requestAnimationFrame`. No step is one blocking call, and the
screen keeps showing the live scene, which gets visibly better while you wait (the "developing" effect).

### 2.2 Where the settings are pushed

Today `GFX.set()` saves to localStorage and turns the preset into 'custom' (gfx_settings.js:435, 582). The shot
must not touch the player's settings, so it gets a **transient override layer** in gfx_settings.js:

```js
const tok = GFX.push({ shadows:'ultra', clouds:'full', water:'full', mirror:'live', bands:'far', drawDist:'full', ... }, 'photo');
GFX.pop(tok);   // back to exactly what apply() had, nothing saved
```

`apply()` reads `Object.assign({}, S, ...overrides)`, and `save()` writes `S` only. GATE GFX gains rows: push/pop
round-trips, pop restores bit-exact `applied{}`, nothing is written to storage, and a preset stays a preset.

A few knobs are beyond any menu row. They get **photo-only levers** next to the rows, never in the menu:

| lever | today | in the shot | how | cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| resolution | drawing buffer = window x `min(dpr, 1.75)` (`app.js:218`); AA `ss` 1 or 1.25 | **2x the window** (memory ladder 2.4) | `renderer.setPixelRatio(k)` + `resize()`: `setSize(w,h,false)` leaves the CSS alone, so the screen looks the same, only sharper and slower; `aa.setSize` follows | reallocates the AA target, mirror and cloud targets (one frame each) |
| sun shadows | 1024-4096; box half-width 105 + reach x 0.55 | 4096 (8192 where `MAX_TEXTURE_SIZE` allows), box fitted to the frustum's visible ground | `WORLD_RIG.set({shadowMap})`; the far cascade refreshes every frame instead of every 4th | reallocation only, IF shadows were already on. From off, castShadow recompiles the lit materials: held frames, waited out |
| water mirror | `MIR.res` 0.4, modes off/auto | `live`, res 1.0, far 8000 | `WATER` mirror setters | 4-6 ms at res 0.5 today, geometry-bound (~11 M tris); at 2x and res 1 maybe 40-80 ms a frame. Fine for 10 frames |
| clouds | `S.mode` half, steps 48, lightSteps 5, temporal tBlend 0.1 | full, steps 96, lightSteps 8 | `CLOUDS.S` | resizes its RTs. The temporal history needs **10-20 frames** to converge after any resize: the reason resolution goes FIRST |
| tree fill | `FILL.budgetMs` 4 | 16-24 ms + `prewarm` round the eye | `TREE_FILL.budget`, `.prewarm`, `.ringReady` | spread over frames, measured: 1.5 km prewarm = 2.4 s at 40 ms |
| far trees (stand cards) | `far` 30 km on the island | as is (already the whole island) | ensure built round the eye: `stand_cards` rows at `rowMs` 8 | incremental already |
| draw distance | `VIS` (far plane 3-100 km + mesh hiding) | `full` | the drawDist row | flag |
| mist | the row (on / land / banks) | banks + relief + patch | ATMO.MIST | uniforms |
| tree density | 100-200 (a ROW) | **200** (ultra's) | `density` in the photo layer: a re-stream of every chunk, ~10 s when the player was below 200 | the user's answer (Q5): in, as part of the high-quality option |
| real trees | `bands` near/mid: full trees to 10-50 m, light rung to 30-120 m | **full trees to 150 m, real trees to 1000 m**, fade 60 m: the night hero's reference (`tools/look1_temporal.js:260`, `GFX.set('bands','far'); TREE_LOD.set([150, 1000, 1000]); TREE_LOD.fade(60)`) | `TREE_LOD.set` / `.fade` | the frame is dearer in a dense stand. That rig WAITED 22-30 s for the trees to stream in (a fixed sleep, not a measure) |
| FAR_FILL / FAR_WOOD | build-time const (render_world.js:378) | unchanged | baked into the impostor materials | not per shot |

Governors are held for the shot: `GFX.hw.hold(true)` (the runtime step-down), `FLYDIY_AA` auto scale off,
`FLYDIY_PACE` uncapped, `DAY_CLOCK.rate(0)`.

### 2.2b The player's machine (the user, 11 Nov: "if someone is having this on potato, then maybe we shouldn't ...")

High quality is OFF by default for everyone (opt-in). When a player turns it on, the layer is scaled to the preset
their machine was built with (`GFX.budget().preset`, the build-time row the page loaded with):

| machine (build preset) | the high-quality layer | the bar says |
|---|---|---|
| gamer, ultra | the night hero's: ultra rows, density 200, real trees to 1000 m, the memory ladder from rung A | - |
| current | the same, the ladder from rung C | "can take up to a minute" |
| retro, potato, laptop | density and the tree bands as they are (their BUDGETS cap the forest), shadows full, clouds full, the mirror off; the picture at the window's size (rung E) | "this computer: slow, up to a minute" |

The per-machine table lives with the layer in `gfx_settings.js` (P4), tested by GATE GFX; the core only holds the cap.

### 2.2c The cap

From the shutter to the file: at most `SCENE_HQ_CAP_S` = 55 s (core, GATE SCENE holds it under 60). Each step has its
own share; when the clock runs out mid-step the shot is taken with what is there, and the pill and the report say so
("trees 82 % at the cap"). A cancel is always immediate.

### 2.3 The steps and the spinner

THE SPINNER sits in the middle of the screen (the user: "the spinner can be in the middle of the screen, frankly ...
it disappears afterwards"). Not verbose, but it must keep a person company for 20 s ("that's very stressful,
especially in a web interface. You have the feeling that things have gone wrong"):
- a ring that FILLS with the overall % (not an endless spin), with the % in it, and a CSS turn on top (it runs on the
  compositor and keeps moving even through a slow GPU frame);
- ONE short line under it that changes with the step, in plain words: "Getting the scene ready", "Sharpening",
  "Growing the far trees · 64 %", "Waiting for the clouds to settle", "Taking the picture", "Saved";
- after 8 s, a second, fainter line: the time so far and the cap ("12 s · at most 55 s"), so a long wait reads as
  progress, not as a hang;
- behind it the live picture keeps getting better (trees appearing, the water clearing), which is itself the show;
- ✕ in the ring's corner cancels.

| # | the spinner says | what runs | done when | budget |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | (flash + click) | shutter feedback; `snapshot()` of everything the pipeline touches: `GFX` applied, the AA tier/ss, the pixel ratio, the mirror, the `CLOUDS.S` fields, the shadow sizes, `TREE_FILL.budget`, the governors, the clock rate | instant | 1 frame |
| 1 | preparing the scene | governors held; `GFX.push(photo)`; the light rows (shadows, mist, drawDist, bands) | next frame presented (not `held`) | 1-3 frames |
| 2 | sharper | the resolution step (pixel ratio, AA ladder, 2.4) | the targets are rebuilt and a frame is presented | 1-2 frames |
| 3 | trees · n % | density 200 if below; real trees to 1000 m; fill budget raised, prewarm round the eye, stand cards round the eye | `ringReady()`, the fill queue empty and no stand-card row pending, OR the cap (below) | spread, 4-24 ms a frame |
| 4 | shaders | anything that recompiled (from shadows-off, tone) | 8 consecutive presented frames with no `held` | waits, never blocks (the shader guard holds frames, as at boot) |
| 5 | water and sky | mirror live + res; clouds full + steps | 20 presented frames (the clouds' temporal history) | 20 frames |
| 6 | rendering | `photoGrab` on the next presented frame; `toBlob` (async; the encode runs off the main thread) | the blob is back | 1 frame + encode ~0.5-1.5 s at 4K |
| 7 | saved ✓ | download + the scene inside the PNG (3.4) + a thumbnail in the scene list | - | - |
| 8 | (back) | `GFX.pop`, resolution back FIRST (the biggest memory), then mirror, clouds, trees, governors, the clock | the next frame presented | 1-2 frames |

Total estimate on the box: 3-8 s without the tree step; WITH the night hero's trees, step 3 is the unknown: the
LOOK-1 rig slept 22-30 s for them, which is a ceiling it chose, not a measure. The real time is measured first thing
in P4. If it is over 10 s, the pill shows the % as it goes, and the fix is to keep the trees loaded across shots
while the bar is open (a second shot from the same place starts at step 4).
A cancel (✕, Esc, a tab hidden, a WebGL context loss) jumps to step 8 from wherever it is.

**"Never freezes", and the browser must never think it has** (the user: "make sure that the browser doesn't interpret
this as a freeze or a crash and asks to reload"). Two different watchdogs can fire, and the design keeps clear of both:
- **The page's main thread** (Chrome's "Page unresponsive - wait / exit" after the page has not answered input for
  several seconds). Every step runs inside one `requestAnimationFrame` and returns; the heavy CPU work (the tree fill,
  the stand cards) is already budgeted per frame (4-24 ms). Rule: no main-thread task over 200 ms during the shot
  (a `PerformanceObserver('longtask')` in the pipeline, its worst written in the report; GATE PHOTOSTEP checks the
  stepper never runs two steps in one frame).
- **The GPU** (the operating system resets a GPU that spends too long on one batch - Windows' TDR is 2 s by default -
  and the page loses its WebGL context: a black canvas, Chrome's "Rats! WebGL hit a snag", or a reload). Rule: no
  single frame over ~0.5 s of GPU. The 2x frame is the risk: the ladder (2.4) times its first frame
  (`EXT_disjoint_timer_query` where there is one, else the frame's wall time) and steps one rung down if a frame
  took over 300 ms, before the picture is taken. A `webglcontextlost` during the shot cancels it cleanly and says so.
- **A hidden tab** stops `requestAnimationFrame`: the shot waits (the clock of the cap stops too) and goes on when the
  tab comes back.

### 2.4 The memory ladder (the 2x risk)

The AA target at 8x MSAA costs about 144 B/px (aa_resolve.js comment, G1250). At 2x of a 1920x1080 window
(3840x2160 = 8.3 Mpx) that is about 1.2 GB, too much for the GTX 660-class machines the presets were cut for. So the
step-2 resolution is picked from a ladder, the first rung that fits a byte budget AND `MAX_RENDERBUFFER_SIZE`:

| rung | drawing buffer | target samples | AA ss | ~bytes at 1080p window |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 2x | 8 | 1 | 1.2 GB |
| B | 2x | 4 | 1 | 660 MB |
| C | 2x | 0 (+ the 2x is itself supersampling against the 1x the eye sees) | 1 | ~300 MB |
| D | 1.5x | 4 | 1 | 370 MB |
| E | 1x | 8 | 1.25 (today's `full`) | 290 MB |

Budget: a 400 MB default (rung C on a 1080p screen), raised by `GFX.hw`'s measured class on a big GPU, lowered on the
phone (rung E). The rung picked and the output size are written in the scene file and in the report.

Bigger than 2x (e.g. 8K for print) would need TILES (`camera.setViewOffset`, 4 tiles assembled in a 2D canvas). Post
effects that read neighbours (bloom, AO, lens, sun glare) and the clouds' history make seams, so tiles are OUT of this
design and listed as a later option only.

### 2.5 Output size (the user's answer, Q1: a choice, the window's size by default)

The bar's size row: **window** (default), 1920x1080, 2560x1440, 3840x2160, custom. The ladder (2.4) then renders the
picture at up to 2x that size and the resolve's tent filter scales it down to the size asked for, so the extra
resolution goes into sharpness (supersampling) rather than into a bigger file. A size with another aspect than the
window letterboxes the screen for the shot (the canvas's CSS box set to the output's aspect), so a 16:9 picture comes
out of any window. The size is saved in the scene (`quality.output`). For the Steam page: 1920x1080 at least, 16:9.

### 2.6 Cost and risk

- Cost: 2-3 sessions. The stepper and its snapshot/restore are node-testable with stub handles, the way GATE GFX
  tests the menu. The tuning (which levers, the frame counts, the ladder's budget, the long-task rule) needs the real
  world on a real GPU.
- **This needs a LOCAL session and a GPU box slot from A0 (the Performance Coordinator)**: SwiftShader draws the world
  black (the PMREM probe) and takes minutes a frame (QUICKSHOT.md, HANDOVER G-entry ~72171), so none of steps 2-6 can
  be judged in the cloud. Ask for `tools/perf/boxlock.sh take gpu photo-mode-2`, about 2 hours, after the Fri-Sat
  round.
- Risks:
  - GPU memory at 2x (the ladder answers it; a context loss during the shot must cancel cleanly).
  - The restore missing a field. One function snapshots and the same table restores, and a node gate diffs the
    applied state before and after.
  - The clouds' temporal history after a resize. Resolution goes first, and 20 frames are counted after it.
  - The mirror at 2x and res 1 being slow (40-80 ms). It is acceptable for 20 frames; the long-task rule measures it.
  - The shadow box fitted to the frustum flickers if it moves between frames. It is fitted once, at step 1, and
    held.

## 3. Saved scenes: one format for the player and for Claude

### 3.1 The file

A JSON document, `what: "wingsmith-scene"`, `v: 1`. Full schema: `futureDesigns/photo-mode-2/scene.schema.json`.
Example: `futureDesigns/photo-mode-2/example_cub_lagoon.scene.json`. The sections:

```jsonc
{
  "what": "wingsmith-scene", "v": 1,
  "name": "Cub over the lagoon", "made": "2026-11-11T18:20:00Z", "game": "L38a",
  "site": "flight",                       // "garage" | "flight"
  "world": { "kind": "island", "seed": 0 },
  "camera": {
    "anchor": "plane",                    // "plane": eye and target in the plane's heading frame (re-pose keeps the framing) | "world"
    "eye": [-9.5, 2.1, 6.0], "target": [0.4, 0.2, 0], "roll": 0, "fov": 38,
    "mode": "free", "level": true
  },
  "time": { "date": "2026-06-21", "localHours": 19.25, "frozen": true },
  "weather": { "cloudType": "cuh", "cloudCover": 0.3, "cloudSeed": 1, "turbidity": 2.8,
               "wind": { "kts": 8, "dirDeg": 250, "gust": 0, "refH": 10, "breeze": 1 },
               "seaT": 1234.5, "clouds": { "detail": 1, "veil": 0.2 } },
  "garage": null,                         // { "mood": 2, "door": "open" } when site = garage
  "plane": { "ref": "stock:cub", "build": { "what": "flydiy-build", "v": 10, "name": "Cub", "spec": { } } },
  "pose": {
    "where": "air",                       // "ground" | "air" | "water" | "stand" (the garage's mount)
    "at": [1520.0, null, -840.0], "agl": 45,           // ground/water: y = null -> seated by seatOnGround
    "hdg": 250, "pitch": 4, "roll": -18,               // degrees; ground: pitch/roll null = solved from the wheels
    "controls": { "elevator": -0.1, "aileron": -0.35, "rudder": 0.05, "flaps": 0, "gear": 1, "throttle": 0.8 },
    "prop": { "look": "blur", "rpm": 2300, "angleDeg": 0 },
    "canopy": 0
  },
  "quality": { "hq": true, "rung": "auto", "output": null, "overrides": { } },   // output null = the window's size
  "note": "Steam #3 - the lagoon at golden hour"
}
```

Decisions inside it:
- **The plane is carried INLINE** (`plane.build` is the garage's own export envelope, garage.js:617). The Cub is
  8.7 KB (3.7 gzipped), the C172 32 KB, so a scene is 10-35 KB, and it re-opens exactly even after the player's slot
  has changed. `ref` is a readable name only. The build's own `v` (GEN_SPEC_V) migrates through `genNormaliseSpec`
  as an import does today.
- **The camera anchored to the plane by default.** `eye` and `target` are in the plane's heading frame (x forward
  along the heading, y up, z right; the plane's pitch and roll do not rotate it), so a scene re-posed or moved keeps
  its framing. `anchor: "world"` is for landscape shots. Roll is new (devCam has none). The rule is `camera.up`
  rotated about the view axis.
- **Time is frozen** in a scene (`DAY_CLOCK.rate(0)` + `set({date, localHours})`). `seaT` pins the waves' phase
  (anchored on `sim.t`, day_clock.js:204-215), so the water re-opens with the same waves.
- **Quality**: `hq` and `rung` drive the pipeline; `overrides` are extra GFX rows on top of the photo layer (a rig
  can ask for `density: 200`). The player's own settings are never in the file.
- Units: metres, degrees, world axes x / y-up / z as `terrainH` reads them, heading as `site.hdg` (degrees in the
  file, radians inside).
- Unknown fields are kept and written back (forward-compatible); a missing field means "leave it as it is", so a
  rig can write a 6-line scene ("this build, this camera, golden hour").

### 3.2 Opening a scene: the one ordered loader

`SCENE.apply(scene)` is a promise, and the ORDER is the fix for "staged before the roll-out reset":

1. **site**: garage or flight. If the scene is a flight scene and we are in the garage, load the build and roll out,
   and await the roll-out's END (its reveal included, the same `BOOT`/phase test plane_shot.js uses, one helper now).
2. **build**: `GARAGE_SPEC.set(build)` (if not the same spec hash).
3. **time and weather**: `DAY_CLOCK.set` + `rate(0)` + clouds dials + `seaT`.
4. **pose** (section 4).
5. **camera** (after the pose, because it is anchored to it).
6. **hold**: `gmHold(true)`, `SHOT_MODE.enter()`.
7. **stream**: the near world round the EYE (not the plane): `TREE_FILL.prewarm`, terrain quads, `ringReady`.
8. **validate** (3.3) and report.

Every step is idempotent. If a reset event comes after the scene was applied (a roll-out, a respawn, a career popup),
`SCENE` re-applies from step 3. For that the page needs ONE new event, `wingsmith:reset`, fired by the roll-out's end,
the respawn and the career's popups (today each rig guesses these from phase words and button texts).

### 3.3 Validation (the eye in the trees, the plane missing)

`SCENE.check()` runs after the scene is applied and after every high-quality shot. It returns a list of warnings that the bar
shows and a rig fails on:

- the eye above the terrain + 0.5 m (`terrainH`), and above the water;
- the eye not inside a tree crown (a query against the tree field's placed instances near the eye; the fill keeps them
  per chunk, and the query is new: P2 checks it can be answered cheaply);
- the near plane: the eye at least 0.8 m from the skin (plane_shot.js's rule);
- the plane in the frustum: its bounding box projected, and its share of the frame (default: at least 2 % of the
  frame, unless `pose.where` is null, which means "no plane");
- the plane VISIBLE: one extra frame with the craft hidden, diffed (plane_shot.js's assert 6), only in rigs and in
  `--check`; too slow for the player;
- not under the boot overlay: `BOOT.state === 'gone'`;
- the ground pose resting: every wheel within 5 cm of the ground (`seatOnGround`'s clearance).

### 3.4 Where scenes live

- **In the game**: localStorage `flydiy.scenes` (a list: name, thumbnail 256 px JPEG, the JSON). The Picture tab
  shows them as cards: Open / Rename / Export / Delete. Import accepts a `.scene.json` or a PNG (3.4c).
- **Export**: `<name>.scene.json`, a download (the same `exportFile` the garage uses).
- **Inside every picture**: the PNG carries the scene in a COMPRESSED `iTXt` chunk keyed `wingsmith-scene` (deflate,
  the browser's `CompressionStream`): 4-12 KB on a picture of several MB. Dropping a
  picture back onto the game re-opens its scene. It is about 40 lines of chunk writing (after `toBlob`, before the
  download: insert one chunk before IEND, with a CRC32). It also means every Steam picture documents itself.
- **In the repo**: `shots/scenes/*.scene.json` (new folder) for any scene an agent or the user keeps (the Steam scenes too, when someone makes them); the
  outputs go to `screenshots/` (already gitignored).

### 3.5 The rig API: the same scene, headless

Page side: `window.SCENE` (in photo.js):

```js
SCENE.ready()                    // -> Promise: BOOT gone, the set loaded, no held frames for 8 frames
SCENE.apply(scene, { hq })       // -> Promise<report>  (the ordered loader, 3.2)
SCENE.get()                      // -> the current scene, as the bar would save it
SCENE.check()                    // -> [warnings]  (3.3)
SCENE.shoot({ hq, output })      // -> Promise<{ png: base64 | blob, w, h, rung, steps:[{name, ms, frames}], longTasks, warnings }>
```

Node side: ONE rig, `tools/scene_shot.js`, replaces the copied staging code:

```
node tools/scene_shot.js shots/scenes/lagoon.scene.json screenshots/scenes/lagoon.png [--gpu] [--draft] [--check] [--page dev.html]
node tools/scene_shot.js shots/scenes/*.scene.json --out screenshots/scenes/   # every committed scene
node tools/scene_shot.js --from-page out.scene.json                     # save what a running page shows
```

It serves the tree (`_serve.js`), launches Chromium (GPU flags with `--gpu`, the SwiftShader flags otherwise),
awaits `SCENE.ready()`, `SCENE.apply()` and `SCENE.shoot()`, writes the PNG and a `.report.json` beside it (eye,
plane share of the frame, warnings, step timings), and exits non-zero on any warning unless `--draft`. `--check`
adds the hidden-craft diff.

What changes for the 53 rigs: nothing at once. New pictures use scene files. The air and world rigs (island, cloud,
water, biplane, float_water, crowd, lake_holes) move first, because they are the ones that fail. The UI rigs
(map_menu, lists, ...) are not pictures of a scene and stay as they are. **QuickShot stays** (node, orthographic, no
world). It gains `--scene file`, reading only `plane.build`, `pose.controls`, `pose.prop` and the camera's direction,
so the same file gives a 3 s draft of the plane before the 3 min real shot.

### 3.6 Cost and risk

- Cost: the format, its loader, the check and the PNG chunk: 1-2 sessions; `scene_shot.js`: 1 session.
- Risks:
  - An exact re-open depends on the world being deterministic. It is for seed 0, the island and the trees (seeded),
    but not for the wildlife, the traffic and the crowd. Scenes freeze what they can and the report lists what moved.
  - The build in the file going stale as GEN_SPEC_V moves. The same migration as a build import; a gate re-opens
    every committed scene (GATE SCENE, below).
  - The career's clock: a career scene cannot jump the clock. In the career, scenes keep the camera, the plane and
    its pose, and take the time as it is (Q3).

## 4. Where the plane is: save where you are, load it back, and a teleport (the user, 11 Nov evening)

The user: "don't get crazy. The idea would just be that you play, and then I find, for example, a nice spot. I just
click save that scene, and I can load a scene, and that's really about it ... no crazy functionality". So there is NO
posing UI: no "put the plane here", no pitch / roll sliders, no Plane tab.

### 4.1 Save this scene
Takes what is on the screen, as it is: the camera, the time and weather, the build, and the aeroplane's state read off
the sim: its CG, its attitude (heading, pitch, roll from `sim.axes()`, in placePose's convention), on the ground or in
the air, its controls (`sim.ctl`), its speed. The pause (photo mode holds the flight) is what makes it a still.

### 4.2 Load a scene
Puts that back: the build (a roll-out first when we are in the garage), then `placePose` (P0) at the saved CG and
attitude, `seatOnGround` when it was on the ground, the controls snapped to their saved values (the link stepped to its
target in one step), the time and weather, the camera. It is the same loader a rig uses (3.2). What is not exact: the
wing's flex (the pose is the as-built shape, rigid; a held frame of a banked aeroplane shows the wing at rest) and the
world's moving things (traffic, animals). Both are written in the load's report.

### 4.3 Go to (the teleport)
The user: "if I have to do it manually ... at least a teleport to different places ... the starting place ... maybe
inside the photo mode". The start place is still in the code: `FLYDIY_ROUTE.spawn(id)` (app.js:16287) sets where the
next roll-out starts (the perf rigs use it); only the player's picker was taken out. The **go to** slot lists the
island's aerodromes and strips; a pick sets the spawn and restarts the flight there (the flight's own Restart path,
the aeroplane on that place's stand), photo mode staying open. The map's own teleport (G3642: a click on an owned
garage) stays as it is.

LOW PRIORITY, later: "point on the map where I want to be" - a map click -> `placePose` on the ground there +
`seatOnGround`, refused on water for a land plane. Not in P1-P5.

### 4.4 Cost and risk
- Cost: part of P2 (save / load) and P3 (go to), about 1 session together; `placePose` is done (P0).
- Risks: the worker mirror path (`SIMW.place` must take a pose atomically); a scene saved mid-air loads held, and
  Done after a load leaves the aeroplane there (a place you chose), still paused, so the flight resumes from it -
  the pilot's phase is re-derived as after a reset (to check in P2).
