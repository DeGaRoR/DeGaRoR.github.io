# SOUND — the whole audio layer, from a blank page (2026-10-04)

Coordinator and architect: the **Sound Coordinator** session. Integrator for sound branches: the same session
(branch `claude/sound-integration`), handing finished blocks to **A0** (the Performance Coordinator) for a train.
G-block **G1600–G1699** reserved for SOUND (A0 confirmed 2026-10-04). Sessions are named `SND-*`.

State today: **nothing exists.** No `AudioContext`, no oscillator, no audio file in `src/`, `tools/` or `media/`
(the only `AudioContext` in the bundle is three.js's own audio classes, which nothing calls). Earlier docs only
*owe* sound: an audio vario (CLIMATE §owed), the tram's sound (TRAM-MOTION §4), "no audio, no ATC" (PANEL),
"sound: none" (TURBOPROP §2), and ARCH-2026-09-27's worker split lists `sound` among the main-thread readers of
the physics snapshot. This document is the first design.

## 0. THE IDEA IN ONE PARAGRAPH

**The aeroplane's sound is generated from the aeroplane.** The solver already publishes the prop's rpm and the
engine's rpm separately, the airspeed, alpha, sideslip, load factor and thrust; the registry knows each engine's
family, cooling, aspiration and reduction gear; the prop knows its diameter and blade count; the build knows
whether the cabin is fabric, metal or open. Those numbers *are* a sound: blade-passage frequency = prop rpm/60 ×
blades, firing frequency = engine rpm/60 × cylinders/2, tip Mach = π·D·rpm/60/c. So the engine and the prop are
**procedural** (a player who builds a new aeroplane hears a new aeroplane, which no sample library can do),
while **nature, ground textures and impacts are recorded** (CC0 field recordings beat any synthesis there), and
**music is curated** free music now, commissioned later.

The thin slice comes first and lands as its own train, because flying with engine sound changes how everything
else is tuned (you hear the prop load up, the stall coming, the autopilot hunting). The full layer follows in
four waves of cloud sessions.

## 1. WHAT WE BUILD ON — we do not start from scratch

### 1.1 The engine synth: a port, not an invention

| Source | What it is | Licence | Role here |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Baldan & Delle Monache, "Physically informed car engine sound synthesis for virtual and augmented environments"** (SIVE@IEEE VR 2015) | the model: each cylinder a waveguide chamber, with intake / exhaust / extractor pipes, valve-timed reflection coefficients, ignition pulses, a muffler of waveguide elements | paper | the theory and the parameter meanings |
| **DasEtwas/enginesound** (github, Rust, 382★) | the reference implementation of that paper: cylinders, pipes, muffler, crossfade loop recorder, RON presets | **MIT** | the canonical behaviour and its presets |
| **Antonio-R1/engine-sound-generator** (github, JavaScript) | a Web Audio port of enginesound, **already an AudioWorklet** (`engine_sound_generator_worklet.js`, `waveguide.js`), plus a WASM build | **MIT** | **our starting code**: ported into `src/viewer/audio/`, licence header kept, credited in CREDITS.md |

What the port gives us on day one: a piston engine voice whose character follows cylinder count, ignition timing
(firing order), pipe lengths and muffler. What we add (§3): the aeroplane's numbers as its parameters, load from
throttle, combustion jitter, misfire on starvation, start / catch / run-down, and the 2-stroke case.

### 1.2 The propeller: from the aeroacoustics literature

- **Selfridge, Moffat & Reiss, "Physically derived sound synthesis model of a propeller"** (Audio Mostly 2017,
  doi 10.1145/3123514.3123524) — a real-time propeller model driven by the blade geometry, built for games/VR.
  Its listening test: as plausible as other synthesis, not as plausible as a recording — which is exactly why
  the prop here is tuned against reference recordings (§8), not shipped from the paper alone.
- **Gutin's propeller noise** (the classical result, any aeroacoustics text) — tonal harmonics at m·B·Ω, their
  levels rising steeply with tip Mach and loading, the harmonic roll-off flattening as the tip approaches sonic,
  directivity peaking a little behind the disc plane and vanishing on the axis.
- **NASA Auralization Framework papers** (Rizzi et al., NTRS 20200002468, 20200011447) — the decomposition into
  BPF harmonics (magnitude + phase) per emission angle, and the rendering stage: spreading loss, propagation
  delay, atmospheric absorption, ground reflection. Our exterior chain (§5) is a cheap version of that stage.
- **Andy Farnell, *Designing Sound*** (MIT Press 2010) — practicals 24 (jet engine), 25 (helicopter) and the
  motors practical: the method for the turbine voice and for the electric motor's whine.

### 1.3 How the big simulator layers it (MSFS, from its SDK docs)

- **Engine = stacked event types**: Combustion (cone heading 180°, i.e. it radiates aft), Propeller (cone heading
  0°), Starter / CombustionStart, Shutdown — the same engine index, gated by RPM thresholds.
- **Inside and outside are separate buses** (`engine_inside` / `engine_outside`; dedicated cockpit buses for
  combustion, jet whine, propeller), with a **VIEWPOINT state** to cross-fade on camera changes, separate reverb
  share-sets inside and outside, and **insulation** parameters for exits closed / open (their example: 12 dB of
  outside sound removed with the doors shut, 0 dB open).
- **Driven by sim variables (RTPCs)**: prop rpm and % of max, true airspeed (wind), body rotation rates
  (fuselage rattles), vertical speed (touchdown severity).
- **Always-play layers**: fuselage rattle; **wind**; **ground** (three touchdown events: centre, left, right).
- **AI aircraft**: no interior perspective, a reduced set (engine + ground), audible to 5 km, two distance tiers
  (≤ 500 m adds surfaces/gear/aero), a voice cap per aircraft, smoothed parameters because they update every 8
  frames, directional cones.

We copy that **structure** — layered sources, two perspectives, insulation, parameter-driven, distance tiers —
and replace its samples with our synth where the aeroplane is generated.

### 1.4 Recorded sound: where, and under which licence

| Source | Licence | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **freesound.org, filtered to CC0** | CC0 1.0 | **the default** for ambience, ground textures, impacts, mechanisms. Previews (HQ ogg) need no login; originals need an account (§7.1) |
| OpenGameArt CC0 packs (e.g. "Park ambiences", Thimras) | CC0 | supplements |
| Sonniss GDC bundles | royalty-free, no attribution, **but not redistributable as raw files** | **local reference only**: this repo is public and served raw from Pages, so a shipped Sonniss file is a standalone redistribution. Never committed |
| any CC / all-rights recording of a real Cub, Jodel, C172, Rotax | various | **analysis only** (tuning references, §8), gitignored, never shipped |

**The rule:** what ships is CC0, or CC-BY with a CREDITS.md row. No NC, no ND (the game may be sold), no AI-generated
audio (inconsistent loops, murky licensing), no FlightGear samples (GPL).

### 2.2 The bus graph

```
                      ┌─ aircraft.ext ── spatial per source (panner, distance, air absorption, doppler) ─┐
 sources (worklet) ───┤                                                                                  ├─ aircraft ─┐
                      └─ aircraft.int ── cabin transfer (insulation, cabin modes) + interior-only layers ─┘            │
 ambience beds + emitters ──────────────────────────────────────── ambience (ducked inside the cabin) ──────────────┼─ master ─ limiter ─ out
 music (<audio> streamed) ──────────────────────────────────────── music (ducked under events) ──────────────────────┤
 interface clicks ───────────────────────────────────────────────── ui ─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Volumes per bus in the settings (master, aircraft, environment, music, interface). A soft limiter on the master
(DynamicsCompressor, fast attack, −1 dBFS ceiling) so nothing clips when everything peaks together.

### 2.3 Threading and the frame budget (A0's contract)

- **All DSP on the audio thread** (AudioWorklet). The main thread only computes the parameter block and posts it.
- **`AUDIO.update(view, dt)` ≤ 0.3 ms per frame and allocates nothing** (one preallocated Float32Array, AudioParam
  `setTargetAtTime` with τ ≈ 30 ms so there is no zipper noise, a message only for discrete events).
- Reads the same **sim view** the renderer reads, so it works unchanged whether physics runs in the worker
  (simw) or inline; the view's 60 Hz snapshot is plenty for audio parameters.
- **Nothing before the first user gesture.** The context is created and the worklet module added on the first
  `pointerdown` / `keydown` anywhere (the game has no click-to-start; the welcome screen's Play, when it exists,
  is simply the first such gesture). Boot timings and the load's heap curve are
  untouched until then. Browsers require this anyway (autoplay policy).
- **`?audio=0`** builds nothing (A/B switch for A0's rigs). The rigs click, so audio does run in the strict gates,
  which is the point: it is measured.
- Tab hidden / paused / loading → master fades to silence over 0.2 s and the worklet is told to idle
  (`ctx.suspend()` after the fade, `resume()` on return).

### 2.4 Memory and bytes (the Friendly Welcome budgets)

- **Music is streamed** through `<audio>` → `MediaElementAudioSourceNode`, never `decodeAudioData` (a 3-minute
  stereo track decoded is ~69 MB of float PCM).
- **Decoded ambience ≤ 24 MB on gamer, ≤ 8 MB on potato / pocket**: mono loops, 20–40 s, stereo-ised by
  decorrelation, loaded by proximity (only the beds the listener can hear), freed when out of range.
- **Shipped audio ≤ 40 MB total** at first, fetched lazily, cached by the service worker's media rule.
- **Format: MP3 for v1** (decided 2026-10-04 while building the asset tools): it decodes everywhere, Safari's
  `decodeAudioData` included, and the box has no ffmpeg (pip cannot reach PyPI from it), so the tools are pure
  node: `audio-decode` (WASM decoders, MIT) and `lamejs` (LGPL, a tool dependency only; nothing it encodes carries
  its licence). Music 128 kbps stereo, ambience 96 kbps mono. Opus in WebM (+ an AAC twin) stays the upgrade if
  bytes ever matter; the crossfaded loops below make the codec's padding irrelevant either way.
- **Loops are crossfaded in code, never at a codec seam**: every codec pads (MP3/AAC encoder delay), so a loop
  plays as two overlapping voices with an equal-power crossfade of 1–2 s. Loops then cannot click, whatever the
  format.
- Potato / pocket: the engine synth stays full (the audio thread is another core and the sound is the aeroplane);
  ambience runs fewer beds, fewer emitters; an "audio: light" option runs the context at 24 kHz.

## 3. THE AIRCRAFT — sources

### 3.1 Piston engine (the port, extended)

Parameters, all from data we already hold:

| Synth parameter | From |
|---|---|
| rpm | `out.rpmEng[i]` |
| load (ignition strength, intake noise) | throttle and the engine's power fraction (manifold pressure where modelled) |
| cylinders, stroke (2 / 4) | the registry row (`family` two/four; cylinder count per preset — see §9 for the gap) |
| ignition times (firing order) | layout: flat-4/flat-6 even firing; inline; radial (odd count, every other cylinder); 2-stroke twin fires every revolution |
| intake / exhaust / extractor pipe lengths | derived from displacement (bigger engine → longer, lower resonances), tuned per family |
| muffler elements | the exhaust/silencer of the build (ROADMAP Phase 2 item 3 when it lands; a default per family until then) |
| roughness | cycle-to-cycle jitter, high at idle and on a cold engine, low at cruise (there is no mixture control in the sim) |
| misfire | fuel starvation / dry tank: the panel arc's burn already stops the engine — it sputters first |
| turbo / supercharger | the blower family (G195): a whistle layer at compressor rpm |

Events: **starter** (DC motor whine + cranking at 100–300 rpm with compression pulses), **catch** (two or three
coughs, a surge, settle to idle), **shutdown** (run-down, prop windmill to a stop), **after-shutdown ticking**
(the exhaust cooling — a few minutes of irregular ticks; one of the most evocative sounds of a GA ramp).

### 3.2 Propeller voice (new)

- **Tonal**: harmonics m = 1..8 at m·B·Ω_prop. Level from thrust/torque loading and tip Mach (Gutin-informed);
  roll-off flattens as tip Mach rises.
- **Snarl**: above tip Mach ≈ 0.85 the upper harmonics rise and the waveform saturates — the "clipped-prop Cub"
  sound, physically driven.
- **Broadband**: blade-wake noise, filtered noise whose centre follows tip speed (Strouhal), level with loading
  (Selfridge et al.).
- **Chop**: amplitude modulation of the broadband (and of the engine heard through the disc) at BPF; depth grows
  with alpha and sideslip (unsteady loading) — the "whop" in a climbing turn.
- **Reduction gearbox**: the engine and prop run at their own rates (a Rotax 912: 5800 engine over ~2400 prop →
  a high engine voice over a low drone; a direct-drive A-65: both locked low). This single ratio is the main
  reason two light aircraft sound different, and it falls out of our data. Gear whine at mesh frequency on geared
  engines (tooth count nominal per gearbox).
- **Directivity** (exterior only): tonal maximum ≈ 10–20° behind the disc plane, minimum on the axis; combustion
  radiates aft and to the exhaust side (MSFS's cone headings 180° / 0°).

### 3.3 Turbine voice (PT6 and future turbines)

Compressor tone stack (gas-generator speed × stage blade counts: three axial stages + centrifugal), combustion
roar (broadband), exhaust, and the prop in **beta** (the low buzz of a PT6 at taxi). Gas-generator speed derived
from power when the solver does not publish it. Scoped with the turboprop validation work (they share a test).

### 3.4 Electric voice

Motor whine at pole-pairs × rpm, faint inverter PWM tones (8–16 kHz), and the prop dominating. Very quiet — the
case where the ambience is heard over the aeroplane, which is a feature.

### 3.5 Airframe, ground and water (procedural first, sample grains where synthesis sounds bad)

| Layer | Driven by |
|---|---|
| **Wind** | true airspeed (level ∝ V^~3 in dB terms, spectrum rising with V); sideslip and alpha add; open cockpit / door removed → much louder (the build knows) |
| **Ground roll** | wheel speed × surface (`GROUND_SURF`: asphalt hum, gravel crunch grains, grass swish, dirt) × load on the wheel |
| **Touchdown** | per wheel (MSFS's centre/left/right), severity from vertical speed at contact; **tyre chirp** on spin-up when the wheel was not turning |
| **Suspension** | compression rate → thumps and creaks; tailwheel rattle on rough ground |
| **Brakes** | squeal at low speed under brake pressure |
| **Water** | hull/float slap per wave contact, spray hiss with speed, the step (planing) change, the wheeled-aeroplane-in-water case |
| **Stall warning** | per aircraft: **reed horn** (Cessna — airflow-driven, so it moans as it starts), electric buzzer, or none (Cub). From the build |
| **Mechanisms** | flap motor (electric) or lever clunk (manual), trim wheel, throttle, mixture, fuel valve, switches, door |
| **Gyros** | attitude indicator spin-up after master on, spin-down after shutdown (the dead-gyro work, CUB-COCKPIT) |
| **Rattles and creaks** | body rotation rates and nz (MSFS's rattles) plus **beam strain from the frame** — the structure creaks when the wing loads, fabric drums on the Cub |
| **Audio vario** (option) | netto climb from the climate field — owed by CLIMATE |

## 4. TWO PERSPECTIVES — interior and exterior

**Listener = the camera.** The perspective is **interior** when the camera is inside the cabin volume (cockpit
view, the interior orbit), **exterior** otherwise; a switch cross-fades over 150 ms (MSFS's VIEWPOINT state).

Every aircraft source feeds **both chains**, and only the active one is audible:

- **Exterior**: spatialised (§5), directivity, doppler, distance, air absorption. Full ground and ambience.
- **Interior**: non-spatial (lightly panned by the source's side), through the **cabin transfer** — computed from
  the build, not hand-set:

| Construction (from the spec) | Insulation of outside sound | Character |
|---|---|---|
| open cockpit / no canopy / door removed | 0–3 dB | wind blast dominates, engine almost exterior |
| fabric over tube (Cub, Jodel-type wood + fabric) | ~6–12 dB, weak high-frequency loss | drumming fabric, loud and bright |
| metal monocoque (Cessna) | ~15–25 dB, strong high-frequency loss | boomy low cabin modes (~80–150 Hz), muffled exhaust |
| door / window opened in flight | insulation drops toward the open case | (MSFS's exits open / closed) |

- **Interior-only layers**: structure-borne engine vibration (low firing-order thump), rattles and creaks, stall
  warning, gyros, mechanisms, the pilot's controls.
- **Headset** option: a further ~15 dB passive cut (more low-frequency cut when "ANR"), the way real pilots hear
  it; default off, offered in the settings.
- **Photo mode and the roll-out cinematic**: exterior, the camera as listener; the cinematic may duck the
  aeroplane slightly under music.

## 5. SPACE — exterior, other aircraft, the garage room

- **Spatialisation**: `PannerNode` (equal-power; HRTF as an option on gamer), distance model inverse with a
  reference near the aircraft size.
- **Doppler by hand**: `PannerNode`'s built-in doppler was removed from the spec, so the radial velocity
  (smoothed) multiplies the synth's frequencies directly (for samples, `playbackRate`). This is the single most
  atmospheric thing an exterior flight sim does — a fly-past — and the observatory (GAME-LAYER §3) is built around
  watching aeroplanes, so it has to be good.
- **Propagation delay and air absorption**: a low-pass whose cutoff falls with distance; delay for distant passes
  (so a far aeroplane is heard where it was).
- **Other aircraft**: the player's aircraft gets the full synth; others get **baked loops** — the same synth
  rendered at init into 4–6 rpm loops per engine type, then resampled by rpm (cheap, and identical in character).
  Tiers like MSFS: ≤ 500 m full layers, ≤ 5 km engine only, beyond: silent. Caps: 2 full voices + 8 baked.
- **The garage room**: a convolution reverb whose impulse response is **generated from the shed's own dimensions**
  (`genHangarBuild`'s HW/HD/EAVE and wall materials → Sabine RT60 → a synthetic IR). The run-up in the shed, the
  birds through the open door, the music in the room — the shed that the hangar work makes parametric gets an
  acoustic that follows it.

## 6. AMBIENCE — its own workstream

The world around the listener, mixed from what the world already knows.

### 6.1 Beds (loops, mixed by weights)

| Bed | Weight from |
|---|---|
| forest (conifer, Southeast Alaska) | WorldCover tree class around the listener |
| meadow / open grass | grass / shrub classes |
| **shore surf** | distance to the coast, the sea state |
| open water lapping | listener on or over water |
| stream / river | river features (when the world has them) |
| village / town murmur | premises zones (town, harbour, village) |
| airfield | inside an aerodrome's fence: windsock flutter, distant generator |
| high wind / alpine | height above ground, wind speed |
| **hangar room tone** | in the garage (with the IR of §5) |

Beds fade with height above ground (≈ silent above 150 m AGL except wind). They matter most where the engine is
off or quiet: the garage, the stand, taxi, electric aircraft, photo mode, the observatory.

### 6.2 Time of day and weather

From `DAY_CLOCK`: dawn chorus → day birds → dusk → night (insects, frogs, an owl). Species chosen for the theme
(THEME-ALASKA-RURAL): ravens, bald eagles, gulls on the shore, **loons on lakes**, varied thrush in the forest.
Wind strength and gusts from the climate wind field drive a gust layer and tree rustle intensity. Rain when
precipitation exists (owed by CLIMATE).

### 6.3 Positional emitters (one-shots and local loops)

Bird calls placed in nearby trees (random emitters around the listener), gull fly-overs at the shore, a dog in
the village, the tram (bell, rope hum, cabins) and the mill, boats (the world ships), everyday vehicles, animals
(the rigged animals of G498). Each emitter is a world object that declares its sound — the same rule as the
premises catalogue: the editor names no asset, the generator owns its sound.

### 6.4 Asset rules

CC0 only (CC-BY with a CREDITS row if a recording is uniquely good), mono loops 20–40 s, loudness-normalised in
`prep.js`, crossfade-looped in code. Every file has a ledger row: source URL, author, licence, date fetched, and
the licence line archived next to the raw file.

## 7. ASSETS AND MUSIC

### 7.1 The pipeline (run on the box — cloud sessions cannot reach the sources)

1. `tools/audio/fs_fetch.js` — searches freesound with the CC0 filter (the public search pages; no API key),
   records id / author / duration / rating / downloads / tags / licence into `assets/audio/ledger.json`, fetches
   the **HQ preview** into `assets/audio/raw/` (gitignored, like every raw external asset). Originals, if wanted
   for a few hero sounds, are downloaded by the user with their own freesound account.
2. **The listening board** — candidates are published as a private page; **the user picks by ear**. (No session
   can hear; selection is the user's.)
3. `tools/audio/prep.js` — node (`audio-decode` + `lamejs`, like `excerpt.js`): trim, mono, high-pass, loudness-normalise (ambience ≈ −24 LUFS, effects by
   class, music −16 LUFS integrated), encode MP3, write `media/audio/` (through `_media_lib`), the CREDITS rows and the committed shipped-file ledger `tools/audio/shipped.json` (the candidate ledger in `assets/audio/` stays local).

### 7.2 Music: the selection

The brief: **calm and relaxing, but not only ambient** — lo-fi, acoustic guitar, folk, piano, quiet orchestral;
no vocals, no strong melodic hooks in the garage (a hook becomes an irritant on the third loop); **little-known**
(no Kevin MacLeod / Incompetech: players recognise it within four bars and it reads "hobby project").

| Artist | Licence | Register |
|---|---|---|
| **HoliznaCC0** (Free Music Archive) | CC0 (check each album's licence line and archive it) | lo-fi, chill — *Public Domain Lofi*, *Ocean Memory*, *Summer Air*, *Nature Spirit*; also traditional American songs (the SnowRunner register) |
| **Rrrrrose Azerty** (aliases **Komiku**, **Loyalty Freak Music**, **Monplaisir**) | CC0 | French; folk, classical guitar, calm game-soundtrack pieces |
| **Scott Buckley** | CC-BY 4.0 (credit: "'Title' by Scott Buckley – released under CC-BY 4.0. www.scottbuckley.com.au") | warm, nostalgic piano and quiet orchestral — for the welcome screen and the long garage evenings |
| Musopen public-domain recordings | public domain | quiet classical piano (Satie, Debussy) — a possible "classical" lane, optional |
| Pixabay Music | Pixabay's own licence (revocable terms, not CC0) | fallback only |

Start with ~20–30 tracks chosen by the user from the listening board. When the game nears launch, **commission
three bespoke pieces** (a garage theme, a welcome theme, a flight piece); the chosen free tracks become the brief.

### 7.3 The player

Contexts: **welcome**, **garage** (the main room — long sessions), **flight** (off by default or very low; a
toggle "music in flight", perhaps only in long cruise), **photo mode / observatory**. Shuffle without repeats,
4 s crossfades, **silence gaps** between tracks in the garage (30–120 s — silence is part of calm), ducking
under events (engine start, a stall warning), a "now playing" line with the credit, and a credits screen.

## 8. TESTING — the ear is the instrument, the node is the gate

- **GATE AUDIO** (`tools/audio/_audio_check.js`, core tier, node only, `--selftest`):
  1. the parameter math: firing frequency, BPF, tip Mach, gear split, cabin insulation by construction, for the
     validated builds (the user's Cub, the Jodel, the Cessna, the Cessna on floats, the twin-582 floatplane);
  2. **offline renders**: the worklet processor run in node under a tiny `AudioWorkletProcessor` shim; an FFT
     must find the firing and BPF peaks within ±3 % across an rpm sweep, no NaN / denormal / clipping, no DC;
  3. the budgets: `update()` allocates nothing (heap delta over 10 000 calls), ≤ 0.3 ms; nothing constructed
     before a gesture; `?audio=0` constructs nothing;
  4. the licence ledger: every file in `media/audio/` has a ledger row with a CC0/CC-BY licence and a CREDITS
     entry where required.
  Every assertion negative-verified (break it, watch it go red).
- **Evidence** (A0's convention): renders as short Opus files + spectrogram PNGs in `reports/evidence/SND-*/`,
  with a note of **what to listen for** and the A/B switch.
- **The Sound Lab** (`tools/_sound_lab.html`, also published as a private page the user can open on any device):
  the live worklet with sliders (rpm, load, cylinders, prop blades, tip Mach, perspective, construction) beside
  the reference recordings, for tuning by ear.
- **Reference recordings** for the five validated engines (A-65/C-90 Cub, the Jodel's, O-320 C172, Rotax 912,
  Rotax 582 2-stroke twin), interior and exterior: fetched for analysis only, gitignored.
- **The user's ear** closes each wave: a short listening session (the Lab and one flight) and a numbered review,
  the playtest cycle as always.
