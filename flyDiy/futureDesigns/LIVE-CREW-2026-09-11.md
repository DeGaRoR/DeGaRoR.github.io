# LIVE CREW — the pilot as an in-game skeleton (2026-09-11)

The user: "we 'bake' the pilot before flight. It will need to remain animated
though — it will need to move with the controls, the stick and the throttle.
We may even want the character to come out of the plane. I'm ok with baking
passengers, but at least the pilot should remain an in-game skeleton,
controllable. Eventually we'll need small animated loops for the figurants, so
we should be able to generalise the use of rigged humans."

## 1. What was true before (the study)

- The flown aeroplane is a SNAPSHOT (`_cage_join.js`): every editor mesh
  baked into flat per-material buckets. Since G210.2 that included the crew —
  a character went through `cageSkinMatrix` (a CPU linear-blend skin) and flew
  as a frozen, unwelded copy of its seated pose (~5x the mesh's own vertices).
- The stick, yoke and pedals were already rigid MOVING PARTS in flight (G240,
  `model.ctlMoves`, turned by the visual linkage). At full deflection the
  knob left the hand; the gap was written in `_cage_crew.js` with a proposed
  cure ("bake the forearm as its own part") that this design retires.
- FACTS THAT DECIDED THE DESIGN:
  1. Editor and flight are ONE bundle (`index.html` inlines `tools/_cage_*.js`
     behind `CAGE_IN_GAME`), so the character instancer, `dress`, the
     animator and the analytic two-bone `ikSolve` were already on the flight
     page. Nothing to port.
  2. The grip anchor is authored as a CHILD of the control's moving group
     (`gripAt(gP, …)`), so a mirror of that group turned in flight moves the
     job's target with it, and the editor's job solver applies unchanged.
  3. The bake's cage→model map is one linear matrix (`inv(mount)`, then
     `(x,y,z)→(−z,y,x)`, then `Rz(beta)`), so anything authored in the
     editor's frame can be placed in flight verbatim.
  4. r128 `SkinnedMesh` (bindMode `attached`) recomputes `bindMatrixInverse`
     from its own `matrixWorld` every frame: the identity-bind instance is
     correct under any parent. The renderer runs `skeleton.update()` itself.
  5. Live is CHEAPER than baked: Ch20 is 27 k skinned vertices against ~100 k
     baked; draw calls equal (one per character-material either way).
  6. Budgets: `index.html` had ~480 KB of GATE MEDIA's 6 MiB left; this cut
     used ~25 KB. TEXTURES are the real ceiling for more people: ~55 MB of
     PNG per character key (4k diffuse + 4k normal, ~170 MB of GPU with
     mips). The pilot's maps were already loaded in flight; a cast of new
     keys is not free.

## 2. The design (landed as G246)

ONE POSE ENGINE, BOTH SIDES OF THE JOIN. The join emits a `people[]` record
per live occupant instead of baking them (G210.2's "one declared exception to
the snapshot"), and the flight side instances the character again and re-runs
the SAME job solver every frame against the SAME moving control groups.

- `_cage_crew.js`: `bareDummy(parent, rig)` (the bone tree, factored out of
  `makeDummy`); `solveGripJob(dum, j, ctx)` (the job closure lifted to module
  scope: pole, palm back-off, grip basis from the shoulder, wrist re-solve
  from the elbow, reach notes); each job records its enclosing moving part
  (`j.ctl`, the first `CTL_MOVING` ancestor of the anchor) and the pole the
  editor chose in the fig's frame (`j.poleFig`, frozen for flight);
  `markLive` stamps `userData.live` on the character's skinned meshes and
  hangs `dum.live = {key, role, idx, s, palm, fist, anim, jobs}`; published as
  `CAGE_CREW.live` and `CAGE_CREW.ik = {bareDummy, solveGripJob, anchorWorld,
  CHAINS}`. The THROTTLE became a moving part in all three builders
  (`edCtl_throttle`: wall and quadrant levers swing `THR_ARC` = 25° forward
  about their lateral pivot, the push-pull rod slides `THR_PUSH` = 0.45 of its
  length into the panel), grip inside the moving group.
- Policy `window.FLYDIY_CREW_LIVE`: `'controls'` (default: whoever holds a
  moving control — the pilot, a co-pilot with dual controls), `'all'`
  (everyone with a character; passengers then wear their idle live), `'none'`
  (the G210.2 bake). A dev flag, not a design row: no PARTS/SAVE surface.
- `_cage_join.js`: skips `userData.live` skinned meshes in the bake; emits
  `people: [{key, role, idx, s, palm, fist, anim, figM, bones, jobs}]` and
  `cageM` (the bake's own map as one row-major matrix). Per job: `ctl`, the
  anchor in the moving part's FLOWN frame (`p` = anchor in model axes less
  the part's pivot, `q`, `grip`), `poleFig`. Plain data; no THREE at module
  scope (the file is `require`d by node gates).
- `50_model_codec.js makeLinkage` carries `thr` with `de/da/dr/flap`.
- `_cage_char.js`: `animStep(inst, now, spec)` — one instance, one moment,
  the body of the editor's rAF `tick` — so the flight loop orders solve,
  transfer, breathe itself. `dress(inst, dum, {from})` reads orientations
  from another skeleton with the same bones (below).
- `app.js`: `buildPeople` (one `crewFrame` group with `cageM` under
  `model.grp`; per record a bare skeleton at `figM` with the solved locals;
  the instance dressed on it; a grip anchor per job under a SHADOW of its
  moving part) and `stepPeople` (after the `ctlMoves` block in `poseModel`:
  shadows copy the parts' transforms, jobs re-solve, locals copy to the
  drawn twin, `dress`, `animStep`). Disposal skips shared skinned geometry.

THE SOLVE RUNS IN THE AEROPLANE'S FRAME, NOT THE SCENE'S — the finding of
the session. Measured on the default build: `model.grp.matrix` is SHEARED by
3.7° (`sim.axes()` returns the body axis and the structure's up, and
xA·yU = 0.065). Every drawn vertex wears that shear invisibly, but a two-bone
solve composes quaternions DECOMPOSED from world matrices, a shear has no
quaternion, and the knee landed a centimetre off the editor's answer. So the
skeleton that is solved is a DETACHED twin in grp-local space (its root has
no parent, so three's "world" is the aeroplane's own frame), fed the
controls' grp-local transforms through shadow groups; the drawn skeleton
under `model.grp` takes the solved joint locals, and `dress` reads the
orientations off the twin (`opts.from`). Verified: the Mixamo hand sits on
the ATD wrist to 0.1 mm at every deflection, and the flown neutral solve
reproduces the editor's to the readout's rounding (0.0923 / 0.1016 /
0.0405 / 0.0416 m wrist→grip against the editor's 0.0923 / 0.102 / 0.0411 /
0.0417). THE SHEAR ITSELF IS NOT THIS CHANTIER'S: it is poseModel's basis
(G54.2), and whether the visual should be drawn through an orthonormalised
basis is a ruling for the flight side.

Measured (probe page, F-PGAR default, Ch20): stick full aft moves the knob
15 cm aft and 5.5 cm down and the wrist→grip reads 0.0859 m (0.0923 neutral,
0.0742 palm: the same 1–2 cm reach margin the editor has for this rig);
full left 15 cm lateral, 0.0901; rudder moves each pedal 3.5 cm, feet follow
(0.0424 / 0.039 against 0.0405 / 0.0416); throttle full moves the knob 5 cm
forward and 8 cm down (35°; hence 25° now), hand follows (0.0985). Neutral
with the idle off: 4e-11 quaternion drift over 2 s. Idle on: four joints
(Neck, Head, Spine2, Spine1) move as in the editor. Cost of the step: solve
×4 0.02 ms, dress 0.08 ms, skeleton update 0.05 ms — under 0.2 ms a frame.
Draw calls unchanged (one skinned mesh for one baked bucket). Screenshots:
side window, neutral / full aft / throttle idle / throttle full.

## 3. The phases still open

- **Figurants (phase 2).** `src/viewer/people.js` on the same instancer:
  `spawn(key, {pos, yaw, clip, phase, speed})` in the WORLD frame,
  `step(t, camPos)` with the `body` layer (`animStep` already takes any
  spec), LOD hide past ~150 m (an impostor of a standing pose is possible
  through the W0c baker but not a first need). The cast declared in
  `25_airfield.js` `SITE` (ONE declared place). New `ANIMS` rows — Standing
  Idle, Walking in place, Waving — the user downloads the Mixamo FBX, one
  table row each, `fbx_to_glb.py --anim`, `char_prep.py`. Root motion = the
  clip's declared speed along a scripted path.
  RULING OWED FIRST: the texture budget. Each new character key is ~55 MB
  of PNG and ~170 MB of GPU. Import-as-is stands for the model; a runtime
  mip floor for SCENERY people (upload the PNG downscaled, the import
  untouched) is a render LOD, not a re-encode — the user's call. Until then
  the cast should reuse the pilot's key plus at most two others.
- **Passengers live (phase 3).** `window.FLYDIY_CREW_LIVE = 'all'` — no code.
  A row for it only if the user wants it in the design.
- **Climbing out (phase 4).** Needs: a door/canopy OPEN state (the hinge kit
  can swing one), an egress path per archetype (seat → sill → ground), a
  seated→standing transition no Mixamo clip provides for a cockpit (blend
  the seated pose to Standing Idle's first frame over ~0.6 s while the root
  translates along the path), then Walking with root motion — and the
  person re-parents from `model.grp` to the world at the sill. 2–3 sessions;
  its own design doc; phases 1–2 are its prerequisite.
- **Owed from before, now visible:** the neck clip for the interior view (the
  live pilot's head is around the cockpit camera; back-faces cull it as the
  bake did); the palm offset from the rig's hand length; the fist opening
  around a real grip; `dress` allocates ~150 small objects a frame (fine at
  one pilot, worth a scratch pool before a cabin goes live).
