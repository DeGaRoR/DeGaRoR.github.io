The ruling (the user, 2026-10-04)

The parts tree drops its two non-aeroplane roots: the reference plane and the hangar (the shed). They move to the rail, or to the top.

  • The tree is the aeroplane. Its heading is the build's name, and selecting the heading shows every row, as the "My Plane" root does today. Its rows are the 8 assemblies and their parts. The two crossed-out rows are what leaves.
  • The reference plane becomes a rail entry with its own flyout. It keeps G573's two sources, 3D model and Blueprint, as a segmented switch. It is display state and never in the spec, so it belongs with "how you look", which is what the rail is for (G78).
  • The hangar becomes a rail entry too. It holds the shed's size, its parts and the mood. The night flyout keeps borrowing its light rows, as today.
  • Phone: the same two entries sit on the view's own rail (REF, ⌂ in the portrait mock-up), each opening a bottom sheet.
  • The mechanism: CAGE_TREE_ROOTS gains place: 'tree' | 'rail' | 'top', and each root says where it lives. A stored selection of ref or shed (flydiy.edPart) migrates to craft.
