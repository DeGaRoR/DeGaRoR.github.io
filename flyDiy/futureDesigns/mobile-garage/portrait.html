What this shows

  • The view stays on top (36 % of the height): the slider's result is visible while the thumb moves. The plaque's three numbers sit in the view. They are the pre-plaque from the birth flow, worked out in the garage with no world. The numbers here are illustrative.
  • Part chips replace the tree column. The tree still exists one tap away (☰). The chips are the parts of the selected assembly. Tapping a part in the view selects it and scrolls the chips.
  • The trunk first (fitted / type / position / size), then N more rows · show for the "more" tier. The expert tier stays off on a phone unless asked.
  • Every row is 56 px: label, value chip (tap to type), − / + (48 px, hold to repeat), and the scale. Only the knob moves the value (a 48 px hit area round it). A finger landing anywhere else on the scale scrolls the list, and never edits.
  • Undo / redo in the thumb zone: one step per gesture.
  • No hover: the help is an i that opens the row's own line, and the amber notes stay inline.

Try it
