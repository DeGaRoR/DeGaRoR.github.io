### Why the vehicle shell and not the aeroplane's solver

`makeSim`'s ground pass rolls `refs.mains` and ONE `refs.tw` (two on twin booms), steers only the third wheel by the
rudder, brakes the mains, and drives nothing (the propeller is the only propulsion); the generator always builds wings
and aero strips (`spec.wings`). The vehicle shell is exactly what the user named: the AI car's rigid chassis, the
DRIVEN wheels through a power curve (forward min(F0, P/v), a reverse gear, engine braking, each driven wheel capped by
traction), Ackermann front steering, springs and dampers on struts, tyres in the friction ellipse. So: the garage DRAWS
the car, `car_sheet.js` MEASURES the drawing (the join's WYSIWYG rule), the shell DRIVES it.

### What the sheet reads

- the four wheels: `CAGE_GEAR.contacts` (the gear layer's drawn axles, metres), front = the two most forward;
- the body: the airframe contract (`CAGE_GEAR.AF`, 41 stations x 24 angles) + every drawn layer inside the track;
- the mass: the build's own LEDGER (`buildGen(joined spec).parts.ledger`) less `wings`, `bracing`, `tail`, plus a
  CHASSIS (120 kg, authored: a GAP, below); the CG along the car from the ledger's moments (x aft of the firewall),
  put on the drawing by the mains' measured station (`zFw = z(rear axle) + gear.x`); the CG height and the inertia are
  estimates (40 % of the body's height; a uniform box), as the conquer's are;
- the engine: `POWERPLANTS[type].engine.powerW` x 0.85 (a gearbox and a live axle).

## 2. Garage gaps (each to become a row, the REF_MATCH rule)

1. **No chassis row.** A car's ladder frame, axles, steering box and differential have no ledger line; `car_sheet.js`
   adds 120 kg (`CHASSIS`). Owed: a `chassis` ledger row when the build is a car.
2. **The join reads one third wheel.** The aeroplane spec (`gear.twX/twY/twR`) holds one centred third wheel; the
   front pair is measured by `car_sheet.js` from the drawing instead. Owed if the aeroplane solver ever needs it:
   `gear.twTrack`.
3. **The drawn front wheels do not steer.** The castor unit (the yawing part the game turns) is built only for a
   centred steerable station; a pair draws as two fixed legs. Owed for the game: each front wheel its own yawing
   unit (the twin-boom tailwheel's precedent: one per boom, G3800).
4. **Car legs.** The legs are aeroplane legs; the swinging link stands in for a wishbone (one arm, not two), its shock
   top capped at the side's mid-height (`shockAng` <= 90). A double wishbone and a higher shock anchor are rows to build.
9. **The radiator nose is not its own livery section.** The bonnet (cowlSkin) is one section, and the markings do not
   reach it at the bonnet's stations - painting the bonnet while keeping the nose polished needs a section split.
10. **Speedometer.** The airspeed dial stands in; a car's reads the wheels.
8. **No car engine.** The custom engine's model is an air-cooled aero engine's (power and mass of a car V8 off by ~1.3x
   and ~0.35x).
5. **No fenders.** The user wants no wheel wells; cycle fenders (a curved sheet over each tyre, turning with the front
   wheels) would be the period look and are a small part of their own.
6. **Seats side by side.** The card keeps the Moth's tandem; a 1930s roadster is side-by-side (`seatLayout 1` + a
   wider body) — a card choice for the user.
7. **No car is a flight.** A car card is `blocked` from the birth list; the game has no drive mode for a garage build.
