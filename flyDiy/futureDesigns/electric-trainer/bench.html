What changed today

  • The bench was flying the base aeroplane's old wing (9.0 m span). It now loads the card through the game's own load door, so the fitted 10.7 m wing flies.
  • Cooling system and HV cabling are part of the motor assembly: 8.6 kW of heat, climb cooling margin 1.8.
  • Packs at the manual's places: behind the firewall and behind the cabin.

Open gaps

  • wing The cantilever wing buckles in the sandbag test at 3.5 g with the glass structure (4.2 g in carbon). The real limit is +4 g, ultimate 6 g. It flies normal circuits; it would break in hard manoeuvres.
  • landing Landing roll more than twice the manual's: the test pilot brakes lightly, and the short-field pass did not complete.
  • drag Glide 12.4 against 15: parasite drag about 0.41 m² against roughly 0.33 m² implied by the manual.
  • rpm No continuous rpm limit, so top speed runs 12 % over the manual's VH.
