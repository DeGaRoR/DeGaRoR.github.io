## What the measurement changed in the design

- **§2.4 stands, and more so.** Buoyancy was never the risk; the slam is
  not one either at the solver's substep, with the panel sizes a float
  actually has (0.07 m², 0.19 m slices). `slamCap` exists and is idle.
- **The slam must be on the UNSTEADY entry only** (heave, pitch rate, the
  water's motion). The steady forward entry over the inclined bottom is the
  planing lift itself (2D+t), and Savitsky's fit already carries it; counted
  twice, the tow porpoised to 20° and left the water at 17 m/s.
- **The transom fade rides on ventilation.** A water-filled separated wake
  keeps its hydrostatic head; only air behind the edge takes the pressure to
  atmospheric. Unconditional, the hull sat 0.20 m deep at 10 m/s on a third
  of its Archimedes and a lone float never crossed its hump.
- **The step ventilates by two conditions**, both smooth: the flow leaves
  the edge (cavity number 2gd/V² < 1) and air reaches it at the chine line
  (the cavity is `hs` deep; a ramp `dVent` on the chine's depth there).
  Before air arrives the step face and a submerged transom carry the wake's
  base suction (`CpBase`) — the "held by suction" of §1.3, as a term.
- **A lone float free to trim above the hump is not a tank convention.**
  The CP sits a quarter of the wetted length behind the stagnation line;
  with the CG 0.25 m ahead of the step the float alone trims to 15°. What
  holds a seaplane's attitude on the water is its tail, so the tow and the
  landing carry one (a Cub's half: K 1700 N·m/rad, C 470 N·m·s/rad at
  16 m/s, scaled V² and V). H4's water-taxi autopilot will meet this first.
- **Radiation damping** is a term the list lacked: without it the settle
  rang at ζ 0.016; with a linear kRad (INFERRED from the 2D heaving-section
  curve at the float's own frequency) ζ ≈ 0.04.

## For H3 — the constants the NACA tank curves must pin

kTr 0.15 (the check's Savitsky comparison: model/his buoyant term 1.1-1.2
at Cv 3, 0.6 at Cv 5), kWake 0.5, dVent 0.05 m, CpBase −0.15, kRad 0.02,
kBeta 0.0163 (his deadrise correction at CL0 0.10), xi0 0.01, Cpl 0.39.
The bench exposes every one as a slider and the tank form (trim held, load
unloading) is `expTow({fixedTrim, trim, Vlo})`.

## Cuts, named

Spray-root wave rise (λ is the still-water one); whisker spray; the
added-mass inertia m_a dVn/dt; current; roll — the tank locks roll and yaw
(a single float with an aeroplane on it has GM < 0; `free` shows the
capsize). Waves are in the model (`gerstner`, the shared-surface shape of
§2.5 / (ap)) and the bench, not yet in the check's bounds.
