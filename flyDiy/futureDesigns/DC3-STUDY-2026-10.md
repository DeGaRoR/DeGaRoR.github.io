# DC3-STUDY - can the garage build a DC-3 that flies? (G3500-G3509, 23 Oct 2026)

Cloud session, node and headless only. Nothing in `src/` changed. Every number below comes from
`node tools/dc3_probe.js` (a tool, not a gate) on master L22d `062c6125`, unless a source is cited.

## 0. The answer

**Not today, and not by widening clamps alone. Getting there is about M-L of work, not a rewrite.**

- **The aerodynamics are ready.** With the clamps widened in memory, the full-size DC-3 (29 m, 89.9 m²,
  11.2 t) gives a stall of 69 kt clean / 62 kt with flaps (real: about 67 / 58), climbs at 6.2 m/s (real 5.7)
  and cruises at 141 kt. It passes the design check, and the solver is cheaper per frame than the fit build.
- **The structure is not.** No DC-3-class build survives being set on its wheels. Three things in the
  structure fail, in this order:
  1. The wing-pair engine mount is sized for a 118 kg Rotax pair. Under 2 × 750 kg R-1830s it buckles.
  2. The member gauge rule is sub-linear in weight.
  3. The cabin is a single lattice bay, 9.1 m long at full size.
- **The pilot can't fly it.** With the mount strengthened in memory, the 18 m build reaches the runway. The
  pilot then cannot rotate it: the elevator sits on its 0.35 stop. It lifts off at 61-65 m/s (1.6 Vs),
  porpoises at 0.2-0.6 m and settles back.
- **The world mostly fits.** HOME 13/31 is 2325 m × 45 m and 02/20 is 1758 m. The works hangar's 35 m door
  passes a 29 m span. Fleet spots (16 m), taxi clearances (validated to 11 m) and the garage camera (fixed
  14 m) do not.
- **Engine-configs is not a prerequisite.** Master already flies the wing pair as a real twin: thrust at each
  nacelle, a lever per engine, a break group per engine. The branch adds nothing a DC-3 needs.

## 3. The first build (`tools/fixtures/dc3_study_2026-10-23.json`)

Born through the game's own path: the metal Cessna's file with the DC-3's rows, then the page's load door
(`tools/_load_build.js`: the editor's layers, the join, the energy layer), then `buildGen`. The join reports
**0 errors**. Drawn and flown agree: tail arm 6.50 m, nothing cut.

**What "as close as the clamps allow" turned out to mean.** Full size cannot be drawn honestly. The second file,
`dc3_study_fullsize_2026-10-23.json`, is that drawing, kept for stills only:

- the join measures a 21.3 m tail arm, 9.76 m cabin and 22.1 m tailwheel station;
- the flown lattice is cut to 6.5 m, 2.6 m and 8.0 m;
- it is 10.5 m long under a 20 m drawing, sits on its nose frame, and the join's ERRS rows say "the drawn tail
  is not the flown tail".

So the fixture is a DC-3 wing and section at 0.62 scale (span 18 m, the envelope's end), with the fuselage
**shortened** until its tail post is inside 6.5 m (2 cabin bays, a short cone). That leaves a stubby 9 m
aeroplane with the right engines.

**Flown** (certified first, as the bench does: `genCertify` → `def.cert`; spawned as the game spawns: `reset` +
`stance` + the strip; the pilot from 43_pilot.js):

1. **Today: it does not stand.** The engine bearer (ENGL-MNTL) buckles 0.25 s after spawn at 1.78 g settling on
   its wheels; prop strike; the pilot rejects.
   - Proof that it is the bearer: the same fixture with a 150 kg engine stands with 0 breaks.
   - The bearer members are 74 mm² for an 1 890 kg engine group, the same section as for the 582 twin's 118 kg,
     and 1.04 m long against 0.36 m.
2. **Bearer patch (in memory: those 34 members' section × 16):** it stands and rolls straight.
   - The tail comes up at 20 m/s.
   - From VRot (37.6 m/s) the elevator sits on the servo's 0.35 stop with the nose level. The "rotate-pull"
     rescue is tricycle-only (`43_pilot.js:3436`).
   - It lifts off at 61 m/s after 503 m, porpoises at 0.2-0.6 m, and touches back nose-down at 67 m/s (44
     breaks: props, nose). "Balked", "rejected-takeoff".
   - With rotate-pull also allowed for taildraggers (in memory): lift-off at 65 m/s after 501 m, peak 0.6 m,
     the same end. **It never reaches 15 m, so there is no circuit to report.**
3. **Gauge patch (members ∝ weight, kScale cap 12, in memory):** the structure snowballs to 6.5 t, the static
   margin goes negative (−0.08), and the aft fuselage breaks at its root settling.
   - That is a fixed-point rule, not a dial: e = 1 is not the fix.
4. **Widened full size (clamps + gauge + bearer, in memory):** the aeroplane above passes the design check, then
   breaks at the cabin's aft frame (S2-S3, "root-bend") 0.14 s after spawn at 1.82 g, with or without the
   certificate stamp.
   - The lattice shows why: the cabin box is **one bay**, S1 at 1.10 m to S2 at 10.20 m. The wing and the mains
     sit inside a single 9.1 m bay.
   - Today the 2.6 m `cabin.len` clamp hides this.

## 5. What ENGINE-CONFIGS changes for a DC-3

Branch `origin/claude/engine-configs-2` (G3490-G3494), read with `git show`.

- **Already on master:**
  - thrust applied at each nacelle (`30_solver.js:2828-2842`), so an engine cut is a real yaw couple; the
    branch's own gate uses the master twin as its control, at > 3° of sideslip;
  - a lever per engine (menu, eng1..eng4 axes, two cockpit levers);
  - engL / engR break groups (`61_gen_frame.js:257`);
  - both wing sections washed, each about its own nacelle (`62_gen_aero.js:120-127`).
- **What the branch would give a DC-3, and doesn't:**
  - per-disc wash. On master one engine out halves the wash on both wings (`30_solver.js:2847`). The branch's
    per-disc wash is gated off for the pair: `genStripsLayout` returns early unless the layout is pushPull /
    tri / quad. Opening it means re-pinning ENGCONFIG's twin hash. Nice-to-have, S.
  - one cowl per nacelle: the pair still splits one cowl (`61_gen_frame.js:2921-2922`).
- **Neither master nor the branch has:** a per-engine key and fuel feed, or a Vmc figure.
- **Conflicts:**
  - none with widening the envelope. The branch's only new clamp is the four's outer nacelle (`genClamp(zo, 0, 6.0)`).
  - its hunks sit near, but not on, the envelope lines, so expect textual merge noise at most.
  - if the twin's default nacelle station moves with a wider span, ENGCONFIG's identity `PIN` needs `--pin`.
- **Verdict:** a DC-3 does not wait for engine-configs. Land it in either order.

## 7. The probe

`node tools/dc3_probe.js [--build] [--only=full|fit|fitGauge|widened] [--bearer] [--no-fly] [--json=<file>]`

- `--build` rewrites the two fixtures through the load chain.
- Each of the three patches (WIDEN, GAUGE, bearerPatch) is applied **in memory only**:
  - WIDEN and GAUGE are string replacements on `tools/flight_core.js`'s text, compiled into the probe's process;
  - bearerPatch edits the built def;
  - the file on disk is never written.
- A full run takes about 4 minutes. The certificate costs 35-50 s per build.
