## 2. What the numbers say

- **Three families, three shapes.** Small ultralight/tail tyres sit on a BIG rim for their size (rim/D ~0.47–0.50, a
  thin band of rubber, W/D 0.30–0.38). GA Type III tyres are rounder (rim/D ~0.27–0.36, W/D ~0.34). Bush tyres are
  balloons on a SMALL hub (rim/D 0.19–0.29, W/D 0.42–0.46) — the hub almost disappears inside the tyre.
- **Bush tyres all mount on the same 6" wheel** (26/29/31"), so the hub does NOT grow with the tyre: only the 35" goes
  to a 10" rim. Today's model scales the hub with the tyre radius; G3548 already scales it with the bead.
- **Tread.** GA and small tyres: 3–4 circumferential grooves on a flat crown, a short plain sidewall. Bush tyres: a
  round crown that runs straight into the sidewall, covered with LOW (3/16–1/4", i.e. ~0.6–0.8 % of OD) wide lugs with
  wide voids; no circumferential ribs. A slick balloon (today's tundra, whTread 1) matches nothing.

## 4. In code (draft for the proposals; tools/_gear_gen.js TYRE + wheel())

Today: `hw` = W/D, `bead` = rim/D (W = 2 hw R, rim radius = bead R). Standard 0.40 / 0.42, tundra (G3548) 0.44 / 0.25,
slim 0.29 / 0.46; every hub piece scales off R (the tundra off `Rh = R bead / 0.42` since G3548).

- **The rim follows a real hub, not the tyre.** Real small and GA tyres sit on 4–6" rims whatever their size:
  rim radius ~ `0.031 m + 0.20 R` fits the tailwheel 8" (0.051), 11x4.00-5 (0.058 vs 0.064), 5.00-5 (0.067 vs 0.064),
  6.00-6 (0.075 vs 0.076) and 8.00-6 (0.087 vs 0.076). So `bead = clamp(0.031 / R + 0.20, 0.26, 0.50)` for the
  standard carcass; `Rh = R bead / 0.42` for every profile (the hub follows the rim, as G3548 does for the tundra).
- **The tundra's hub is the 6" wheel** for 26–31" and the 10" for 35": rim radius `0.0762 + max(0, R - 0.40) 1.1` m,
  `bead = clamp(that / R, 0.18, 0.30)`; `hw = clamp(0.46 - (R - 0.33) 0.6, 0.42, 0.46)`.
- **Standard W/D 0.35** (from 0.40); grooves by size: 2 under R 0.12, 3 to R 0.25, 4 above.
- **Tread per carcass, whTread ignored** (G3852): standard/slim = the ribbed grooves, tundra = low chevron lugs
  (~20 pairs, height ~0.008 D, bars from the centreline onto the shoulder, slanted so a pair reads as a V, alternating
  sides), on the torus surface (tyreTorus). whTread stays in every file as loaded: it is a hashed cage row
  (bench.js benchStripCosmetic keeps every non-default cage row), so it is read and ignored, never rewritten or
  stripped - procure keeps writing 1.
- Display only: s1R/s2R/twR (what the flight reads) never move; no PHYSICS_V, no certificate withdrawn.

Sizes to render (R in m): tiny 0.07 (5.5"), tailwheel 0.10 (8"), 11x4.00-5 0.135, 5.00-5 0.18, 6.00-6 0.222,
8.00-6 0.28, tundra 26" 0.33, 29" 0.368, 31" 0.394, 35" 0.445.
