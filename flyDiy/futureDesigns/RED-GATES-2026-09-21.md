## 3. The pilot's side — three gates, one pilot (`makePilot`, 43_pilot.js)

### SEAPLANE — three layered causes, two bisected

- The crosswind take-off is not a 35.8° swing any more: it is a WATER LOOP — hdg −20°
  at 3.0 s, −176° at 4.0 s, V 7.2 → 2.5 m/s, water rudder on its stop the whole run.
  The gate's case then wanders 120 s on the water at ~120 ms/step and the runner's
  30-min spawn timeout cut it (the calm circuit passes: 8.8 s off, touch on the lane,
  stopped 282 s).
- Bisected on built cores: 8f8d1884 … 14e66481 (G438) all 38.7° at 6.4 s; 4f69c24c
  (G439 build, sources = G435 only in src/core) loops. On that core, reverting the
  three `|| highThrust` lines of G435 ("a high thrust line rolls three-point and asks
  the water stick") restores 38.7 exactly; reverting the shapeMatch re-centring
  changes nothing. The float fixture's wing-pod engines sit THRUST_ARM 0.548 m over
  the CG (> 0.30), so on the water the pilot holds the stick back from t = 0.
  FIX (high): the hydro rule (G396.4 neutral through the hump) wins over the
  high-thrust rule when `sim.hydro`.
- On HEAD the same revert STILL loops: G445's mass chantier moved the float card's CG
  6.8 cm (x 0.997 → 0.929) while the saved fixture's floats stayed (bow/stern 3 mm).
  A second cause, the CG against the step — float placement / the water arc's re-tune,
  not a bless (low-medium).
- Underneath both, the original 35.8 → 38.7° over the 30° bound (G429/G430 mass and
  drag): the water-rudder loop's re-tune the G431 hand-off named.
