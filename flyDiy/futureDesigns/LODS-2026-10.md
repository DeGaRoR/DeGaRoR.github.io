# LODS 2026-10 — THE FAR BUILDINGS: AN AUDIT, A COMPARISON SHEET, A CLEAN LADDER

The user, 27 Oct 2026, on a still of the new meteo station on Mount Ardent seen from the air:

> "the LODs are not great, they don't even represent closed volumes. Overall, I'm not sure LODs are great. Some
> houses show without roof and black for example. Can we look into LOD generation and do something real clean here,
> with a comparison sheet? It's a real issue. The station model looks good otherwise."

A study plus node tools. **No game code changes.** Paths are relative to `flyDiy/`. Line numbers are from master
`d7e181f4` (train L25c). The meteo station is read from `origin/claude/meteo-code` (not merged yet).

**The sheet:** [reports/evidence/LODS/index.md](../reports/evidence/LODS/index.md) (16 buildings, one PNG each, plus
`index.png`, the one-page overview). **The whole island's numbers:** [audit.md](../reports/evidence/LODS/audit.md)
(98 buildings: every keyed building Jolene places, the village presets as plots, the outbuildings, the 33 town-kit
archetypes).

## 0. THE SHORT ANSWER

The user is right, and it isn't one bug. There are four, and they stack on the same buildings:

1. **No named building has a far mesh.** Every building the island places by key (all 105 of them: the clinics,
   schools, churches, stores, cabins, the HOME hangars, the tower, the meteo station) is a *site item*.
   `premises_build.js genItem` (:447) builds it **at lod 0 only**. Its far rung is `render_premises.js detailOf`'s
   `keep` (:1909): **the 3 largest "plain" bags of the near mesh, by surface area** (`DETAIL.keep2`). That rule picks
   by size, not by what a bag is. So:
   - when the roof is not one of the 3 biggest bags, **the far building has no roof**. The school (×4) keeps
     `siding2+floor+stone`: 2 % of its roof survives, and seen from the air it is an open box with a floor. Also roofless:
     the net store (`deck+trim+siding`: the purlins without the sheet), the wood shed, the outhouse, and partly the
     fish shacks, marine supply, fuel and bait, bunkhouses, tram stations and the meteo station (77–87 %).
   - what it keeps is the **near** walls, **pierced** for windows and doors, without the glass and doors that filled
     them. You can see into and through the building. The long hangar at HOME loses its doors (hole 7 %); every
     BIG_GEN building shows its window holes (4–9 %).
   - **the meteo station** keeps `pane+dark+roof`: the three roofs floating over nothing. The stone ground storey,
     the copper skirt and the core are gone (15 % of every view looks *into* it, 14 % through it). That is the
     still the user sent.
2. **The far colour is ~5× too dark.** The far town's plain material colour is *material colour × the map's mean ×
   `HLOD.gain` = 0.2* (`render_premises.js` :1934, :2286). Under the same light the sheet measures every plain far
   rung at **0.15–0.5× the near mesh's luminance**: black. At gain 1.0 the same rungs measure **0.85–1.1×**. The city
   pack's far shells (`city_cook.js` `LOD.farGain` 0.2) measure 0.37–0.54×. Their colours are sRGB bytes read as
   linear, so they need a decode, not a gain: gain 1 alone overshoots to 2.4–2.9×.
3. **The coarsest rung is a box, not a building.** Past 1.2 km (`HLOD.far2`), `hlodBoxes` (:2321) and the kit's
   `boxLod` draw a flat-topped box at 85 % of the height. It has no roof shape, it carries the same 0.2 gain, and with
   that gain it renders black. The city pack already has the right thing: `city_cook.js shellOf / shellFaces` (:165),
   walls to the eave plus the roof family's prism, closed, 10–14 triangles.
4. **Big site items swap far too close.** Only banded (lod 1) houses use the projected-size edge
   (`R × F / 48 px`, G1395). An item's cell switches at a fixed `HLOD.near` = 150 m. The meteo station
   (R 32 m) drops to its broken far rung when it is **634 px wide** on a 1080p screen; the long hangar at 417 px.

**What already works:** the sampled village houses (plots). They are built at lod 0 and lod 1 together
(G800), banded (G801), on the town shader (G1395). Lod 1 is roofed (97–100 %), closed (hole ≤ 2 %) and as bright
as near (0.8–1.0×). The Metlakatla kit's lod 1 is the same. **The fix is to give every building that path**, and
to replace the box with the city's prism. Every generator already has a lod 1 builder. Built from it, the 105 site
buildings' far rung drops from **318,704 to 115,946 triangles (−64 %)**, and all but three come out closed and
roofed by the gate's numbers (the green column on every sheet). The three: the wood shed and the outhouse sit at the
3 % line (S4 closes them), and the WWII tower is a lattice, open by nature.

## 2. THE AUDIT (node: `tools/lods_audit.js`, `tools/_lods_lib.js`)

Each rung is rebuilt by the game's own rule (restated in `_lods_lib.js`, its constants read from
`render_premises.js`), then software-rasterised from 25 views (8 azimuths × 12/35/60° + straight down). The same
visibility the GPU computes:

- **leak**: the share of covered pixels whose nearest surface is a *back* face. The eye looks *into* the building
  (back-face culled, that pixel draws an inner face lit from the wrong side: black).
- **hole**: where the near mesh shows its outside, the rung shows something more than max(1.5 m, R/10) *behind* it.
  This covers the far wall's inner face through an opening, the floor of a roofless box, the sky through a missing
  storey. A see-through hole shows front faces, so `leak` alone cannot see it (`--selftest` proved that).
- **roof**: from straight above, the share of the near mesh's roofed plan (> 1.5 m over the base) where the rung's
  visible surface is an up-facing front face at ≥ 60 % of the near roof's height.
- **iou**: silhouette parity with the near mesh. **open**: welded edges used once, in metres. A building is an assembly
  of closed slabs that interpenetrate, so this one is informational: no manifold test passes a real building, but a
  picture test does.

The worst offenders (all site items, all at 150 m):

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| house/school | 4 | siding2 + floor + stone | **2 %** | **54 %** | 0 % |
| shed/net store | 1 | deck + trim + siding | **25 %** | **19 %** | 0 % |
| house/tram top station | 1 | siding + floor + steel | 86 % | **26 %** | 0 % |
| meteo/meteo station | 1 | pane + dark + roof | 83 % | **14 %** | **15 %** |
| house/tram base station | 1 | roof + floor + siding | 77 % | 12 % | 0.8 % |
| shed/wood shed | 1 | trim + siding + deck | **38 %** | 9 % | 0 % |

a low angle you see the roof plate's underside and the open wall top. That is cheap to close (§4, S4).

## 3. THE COMPARISON SHEET (`tools/lods_sheet.js`, `tools/_lods.html`, `tools/_lods_rig.js`)

Headless Chromium on SwiftShader, the repo served by `_serve.js`. **The far rungs are drawn by the game's own code:**
`_lods_rig.js` lifts `detailOf, lod1Bags, meanOf, hlodMerge, hlodBoxes, lodMat, farGain` and their constants out of
`render_premises.js` at load. If the rule changes, the sheet changes with it. Each sheet has three rows:
**A** close from the air (az 35, el 25), **B** the far side steeper (az 215, el 55), and **C** the rung *at its switch
distance through the game's own lens* (46°, 1080 rows), its true pixels blown up. Light ratio = the rung's mean
luminance over the near mesh's, same camera. Item sheets carry a fourth, green column: **the same generator's own
lod 1**, the proposed fix.

Sheets: meteo station · school · clinic · log cabin · church · net store · club hangar long · field shed · warehouse ·
WWII tower · village plots (shore cabin, saltbox farmhouse, gambrel barn) · an outbuilding (storage shed) · two
Metlakatla kit archetypes (lod 0 / kit lod 1 / kit box / city far shell). All in
[reports/evidence/LODS/](../reports/evidence/LODS/index.md).

Light ratio, camera C (game today → at gain 1.0, the calibration run `--gain 1`):

| rung | today (0.2) | gain 1.0 |
|---|---|---|
| item far (keep-3), 10 buildings | 0.15–0.48 | 0.87–1.6 (the WWII tower, a lattice: 2.4) |
| item box | 0.12–0.40 | 0.6–2.4 (flat top in the sun) |
| plot lod 1, plain fallback | 0.14–0.31 | 0.83–1.5 |
| city far shell | 0.37–0.54 | 2.3–2.9 (sRGB bytes read as linear) |
| plot lod 1 on its finish (the game's normal far) | 0.82–0.96 | (no gain) |
| **proposed: item's own lod 1 on its finish** | **0.84–1.12** | (no gain) |

*Caveats.* The rig's light is a sun, a hemisphere and a gradient environment, not the game's light rig. It is the
same for every column, so the *ratios* hold. The town shader's far variant (house_tarr) is drawn as the lod 1 bags
on their own finish materials (the same maps and paint). There are no shadows. The kit box column is approximate
(the kit's role shader is not loaded; it wears the finish's siding/roof).

## 4. THE CLEAN LADDER (extend HLOD, don't build a parallel system)

One ladder for every building, plot, item, outbuilding and kit house, on the machinery that already works for
plots:

| rung | what | from (the projected-size rule, G1395) | cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R0** near | lod 0 (TARR bake) | — | 1–36 k tris (per house) |
| **R1** far town | **the generator's own lod 1**, banded, on the town shader (`hlodFarTarr`) | `R·F/48` px (60–600 m) — **items too** (no more fixed 150 m) | 0.3–6 k tris; **no draw of its own**: merged into the cell's town meshes (plain/glass × side) |
| **R1s** shells | R1 minus its detail: the generator's declared **shell roles** | `R·F/9` px | 70–300 tris |
| **R2** silhouette | a **closed prism**: walls to the eave plus the roof family (city_cook `shellOf / shellFaces`, generalised), or the generator's own `far()` hull | `max(far2, R·F/4 px)` | 10–40 tris, one merged mesh a cell |

The rules every rung must meet, held by GATE LODS (§6):

1. **Closed.** Roof ≥ 90 % from above, hole ≤ 3 %, leak ≤ 3 % (25 views). Built, not decimated: lod 1 is the
   generator's own construction (the repo's BUILD, DON'T RECONSTRUCT rule, `_house_gen.js` :26). It is already closed
   on every generator measured. The residual eave leak goes with S4.
2. **Coloured from the real materials.** Far albedo = linear(map mean, `TEX_MEANS`) × linear(material colour or
   paint) × the bag's mean baked AO, **gain 1**. No fudge factor: `HLOD.gain`, `gainItem` and the cook's `farGain`
   go. The city cook decodes sRGB before it writes, or the shell shader does. The sheet's light ratio must sit in
   0.85–1.15 for every rung (a sheet row, not a gate: it needs a GPU).
3. **One ladder, by projected size,** for everything. The hard 150 m switch for items and the hard box switch go.
   The band dithers R0↔R1 as it does for plots today.
4. **Shell roles are declared, not guessed.** Each generator exports `SHELL` (the roles that stay past R1s), replacing
   the house-only `LOD1_SHELL` regex. HOUSE: siding/roof/stone/log/floor (+ siding2/3, roof2/3). BIG: wall/plinth/roof.
   HANGAR: wall/stem/roof/door. METEO: roof/copper/dark/shaft/stone. TOWER/NAVAID: open lattices (no closure rows;
   their far is their lod 1 whole).

## 5. COST AND SAVINGS

- **Triangles (far):** Jolene's 105 site buildings go from 318,704 (keep-3 of lod 0) to 115,946 (their lod 1):
  **−64 %**. Per building: meteo 12,150 → 6,120; school 4,588 → 1,520; clinic 5,590 → 1,372; log cabin 3,068 → 848;
  church 2,624 → 879; net store 1,776 → 298. Hangars and BIG_GEN go up a little (the doors and windows come back):
  club hangar 488 → 564, warehouse 348 → 538.
- **Draws:** items stop drawing their own per-cell far mesh (`meshH`) and box (`boxH`). They ride the cell's town
  meshes, as plots do. That is −1 to −2 draws per cell holding items, and nothing per building.
- **Box → prism:** 10 → 10–14 tris a building. Same draws, now roofed.
- **Build time:** one more `GEN.build(P, 1, F)` per item: ~15 % of its lod 0 build (G800's measure for houses),
  under the loading screen or in the house worker, as plots already do. Memory: lod 1 arrays CPU-side, as plots.
- **Colour fix:** zero cost (a constant per bag at the merge).
- **What it does NOT cost:** no new system, no new shader. The far town's banded material, `hlodFarTarr`, the ring
  and the per-cell bake are reused as they are.

The perf A/B is A0's to book on the local box, per step: frame time over HOME and a Metlakatla flyover at 300 m,
draw calls, `stats.farTris`, boot to reveal. Never read a frame time off SwiftShader.

## 6. THE GATE (added: GATE LODS, `tools/_lods_check.js`, core tier, ~25 s)

For every far rung of every building the island places, made by the game's rules: **roof ≥ 0.90, leak ≤ 0.03,
hole ≤ 0.03**, plus **gain**: `HLOD.gain` and the city's `farGain` ≥ 0.5. Negative-verified: `--selftest` takes the roof
off, opens a wall, winds a lod 1 inside out, and each must go red.

Today's game fails 86 rows, so the gate is a **ratchet**. `tools/_lods_known.json` holds today's failures at their
numbers. A new failure, or a known one getting worse, is red, and a ledger row that starts passing is reported. Each
step below empties part of the ledger; after S5 the ledger is empty and `--strict` is the gate. A new generator, or
a preset whose roof is not among its biggest bags, cannot ship a roofless far rung again.

## 7. THE ORDER (each step its own A/B; the colour first, it is the most visible and the cheapest)

- **S1 COLOUR.** `hlodMerge`: far albedo = linear mean × linear colour × mean AO, gain 1 (drop `HLOD.gain` /
  `gainItem`, keep `HLOD.dirt`'s pull if the sheet still wants it). `city_cook.js`: write linear (or decode in the
  shell material), `farGain` 1. Re-run `lods_sheet.js`: light 0.85–1.15. GATE LODS gain rows flip.
- **S2 ITEMS ON LOD 1.** `premises_build.js genItem` builds `GEN.build(P, 1, F)` when `C.lod1 && C.game`, as
  `genHouse` does; the worker packs it (`packBuilt` with the lod-1 filter, :525). `render_premises.js` gives the item
  `userData.lod1 = lod1Bags(GEN, P, F, R.lod1)`, so `banded()` is true and the item joins `hlodFarTarr`. `detailOf.keep`
  stays only for what has no lod 1 (props, parks, objects).
- **S3 OUTBUILDINGS BANDED.** The outbuilding's grp gets `userData.lod1` from its own lod 1 build.
- **S4 SHELL ROLES + LOD 1 CLOSURE.** A `SHELL` export per generator replaces `LOD1_SHELL`. At lod 1 the walls get
  `capTop` (2 tris a wall), and an eave closure goes under the overhang (a soffit quad per eave), which takes the
  residual 1.5 % leak out.
- **S5 THE PRISM.** `hlodBoxes` → `hlodPrisms`, from `city_cook.js shellOf / shellFaces` (moved to a shared module
  both read), measured on lod 1's roof roles. The kit's `boxLod` becomes the same prism. A generator with no
  rectilinear footprint (the meteo station, the tower) exports `far()`.
- **S6 ONE EDGE.** Items, outbuildings and banded houses all take `lodEdgeCPU(R)` / the shader's `R·F/48`. The cell's
  `rMax` / `rMin` include items. The fixed 150 m goes.
- **S7** `--strict` becomes the gate; the ledger is deleted.
