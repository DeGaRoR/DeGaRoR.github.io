# THE CLIMATE CHANTIER — one wind field, every consumer derived (plan, 2026-09-22)

The user: *"wind, gust, storm, temperature management, accurate wind profile on the mountains
and coasts, proper atmosphere gradient of pressure and temperature, possibly humidity. Link all
that to future haze, and to ripples and waves in the newly constructed water system. Also link
that to the cloud system to drive the movement of clouds. Let's try and have an holistic climate
manager. And would we be able to fly sailplanes with these new settings? Could we do ridge riding
for example? … it is very clear that the game becomes heavy both on CPU and GPU, so we need
versatile, elegant and lightweight methods. We will probably need some sort of debugging
visualization with current lines and intensity. Can we also have hot air lifting things up, or is
this too much of a leap? We need to link all systems first."*

Decisions taken with the user (2026-09-22): **soaring first**; the thermals lift the aeroplane and
bend the smoke and aim the socks (diegetic birds and dust devils owed); a **WEATHER panel on both
rails** (the `#selCond` CONDITIONS select re-pointed, not retired — GATE CLOUD scans it).

## 1. What was found (every line verified in source)

- **The plug the roadmap always pointed at is real.** `world.wind(x,y,z,t)` (20_world.js G72) was
  a uniform base × a power-law shear on AGL (`refH`, capped 300 m) + four deterministic advecting
  gust sines, returning a SHARED scratch vector — `W0`, an exact zero, when nothing is set (the
  fast path every calm gate stands on: GATE WORLD checks the two calls return the SAME array).
  The solver samples it once at the mean wing, then **per wing strip, per tail strip, per
  fuselage blob** (30_solver.js aeroPass) — so an updraft raises a strip's alpha and a spanwise
  gradient produces roll **for free**. Thermals and ridge lift need no solver change; only the
  field.
- **The DAY is the one keeper** (07_day.js; the user's own rule from SKY S1: EXTEND THE DAY, NEVER
  ADD A SECOND ONE). It declares `oatC | dISA`, `qnhPa`, `rh | dewC`, turbidity, cloud cover /
  type / decks, and DERIVES the sun (06_solar), `cloudBase = 125·(T − Td)`, `visibilityKm` (read
  by nothing). `set()` stores unknown keys and `spec()` returns them, so new weather fields
  round-trip with no change to it. Wind was NOT on the day (setDay forwarded it to setWind).
- **The atmosphere** (05_atmos.js) is one constant-lapse ISA with two knobs; `sigma` is PRIMARY
  (sea level exactly 1 — an invariant); dry air declared.
- **Every render seam exists but is a stub**: the clouds drift on `world.wind(0, base, 0, 0)·secs·1.5`
  (clouds.js) — a POSITION, so any wind change slides the whole sky; the mist's rh law is owned by
  atmo.js every frame; the sea is rebuilt from the wind inside setWind (`seaFrom`, a fixed LCG
  seed: continuous in A, but a change of L or dir re-aims 32 trains — `waterH`'s phase is
  `k·(d·x) − ωt + φ`); the ripples, the whitecaps and the windsocks take ONE global vector through
  `WF.setWindVis(base)`, called only by the CONDITIONS select; trees, impostors and grass have no
  sway; the spray has no wind.
- **Cost.** `wind()` is called ~(20 strips + blobs + 1) × 24 substeps per frame; the ground programs
  sit at 14–15 of 16 samplers (no field texture on the ground, ever).
- **The sailplane.** The `motorglider` archetype exists (13 m, AR 11; 15 m "won't climb" on its
  sustainer — a separate debt); the machine sheet has Vbg, Vms, LDbest, sinkBg, sinkMin; no
  variometer, no engine-off flown glide; the pilot has HDG / TECS / `deadThr` (the GLIDE law).

## 2. The answers

- **Sailplanes: yes.** Ridge lift on a 10 m/s wind over a 20° smoothed slope is ~3.4 m/s at the
  surface and ~1.3 m/s 300 m above it — several times a 13 m glider's 0.7 m/s sink. A thermal at
  w* ≈ 2 m/s (Deardorff) with a 130 m core lifts it at ≥ 1 m/s net. GATE SOAR flies both, engine
  off, with the pilot's own GLIDE modes.
- **Ridge riding: yes** — K1, the first physics session after the skeleton.
- **Hot air lifting things: not a leap.** A thermal is one term of the same vector field the craft,
  the smoke, the socks and the debug streamlines all sample.
- **Cost:** ≈ 55 µs/frame of physics reads (budget 0.1 ms), 0 when no rich term is on; the render
  links ≤ 0.15 ms CPU; the sway ≤ 0.1 ms GPU; the debug view ≤ 0.6 ms CPU and only when on; the
  relief raster 50–150 ms, lazily, on the first rich spec.

## 3. The architecture — one field, every consumer derived

```
DAY (declared: wind{kts,dirDeg,gust,refH,…,terrain,breeze,thermals}, storm, lapse/mixH/inversion, diurnalC, seaTau, cloudSeed)
 │ derives on every recompute(): sun, oatC_eff, qnhEff, storm{I,phase,…}, cloudBase (quantised 20 m only while moving), airKey
 ▼
CLIMATE (src/core/09_climate.js, pure)              RELIEF RASTER (lazy, 200 m cells, 12 Float32 channels)
  wind(x,y,z,t)   ← THE SOLVER'S (legacy verbatim | linearised rich)
  sample(...)     ← everyone else — never the cache
  atmosFor(air)   → 05_atmos layered profile (T, p, rho, rh, Td)
  aloft(h), thermals(t), surfaceWind(), cloudDrift(i, utc), haze(), tick(dt, simT)
 │
 ├─ 30_solver (unchanged)     ├─ 43_pilot windAt/gustAt (unchanged)    ├─ 25_airfield runway-in-use (unchanged)
 ├─ SEA: seaFrom split draw/apply, phase-corrected, smoothed by seaTau ├─ 32_hydro (unchanged, reads waterH)
 └─ CLIMATE_LINK (src/viewer/climate_link.js, per frame) → pub {surf, gust, cam, haze, sea, aloftD, veilD}
      ├─ clouds.js drift per deck   ├─ atmo.js MIST (follow)   ├─ water.js setWind (on change)   ├─ socks per sock
      ├─ spray / smoke velocity     ├─ trees / cover / impostor uWind (sway)
      ├─ weather_ui.js (both rails) + F8 climate fold + ?wind= / flydiy.day
      └─ wind_vis.js (streamlines, intensity carpet, thermal columns) + the netto variometer
```

**Four rules, written into the module's head.**
1. TWO CLOCKS. Fast state (the gusts, a thermal's age, the sea's phase) on the SIM clock `t`; slow
   state (a front, the diurnal swing, the thermal potential, the clouds' drift) on the DAY clock
   `day.utc`, which only the viewer advances — a gate holds it. Never the wall clock.
2. `wind()` IS THE SOLVER'S; EVERYONE ELSE `sample()`s. The linearisation cache is keyed on `t`; a
   foreign caller at the solver's instant would re-centre it under the aeroplane's feet.
3. BIT-IDENTITY BY CONSTRUCTION. The G72 field moved into 09_climate VERBATIM as `windLegacy` and
   is the whole field whenever the spec names no rich term. No calibrated gate names one.
4. THE DAY DECLARES, THE CLIMATE DERIVES.

## 4. The module contract — `src/core/09_climate.js`

```
CLIMATE.make({ terrainH, surface, SURFACE, bounds, day, geo, typeAt?, coastAt?, seed })
  .setWind(spec | null) → { base, spd }      // the legacy adapter + the rich fields; mode 'zero' | 'legacy' | 'rich'
  .wind(x, y, z, t)        // shared WV / W0; legacy verbatim, or the reference + Jacobian linearisation
  .sample(x, y, z, t, out) // the full field into `out`, never the cache
  .reliefAt(x, z, out)     // the raster's twelve channels, bilinear
  .surfaceWind()           // the 10 m wind the sea and the socks read
  .ensureRelief() / .relief / .mode / .spec / .rich / .stats / .version
  — K2: .atmosFor(air)  K3: .thermals(t)  K4: .aloft(h) .cloudDrift(i, utc) .haze() .tick(dt, simT)
```

**The spec** (a DAY key `wind`; setDay forwards it; day.spec(), the `flydiy.day` pref and `?wind=`
round-trip it):
- legacy `{ base:[wx,wy,wz], gust, refH, alpha }` — G72's shape, exact;
- declared `{ kts | mps, dirDeg (FROM, true north; the grid's convergence applied as sunAzGrid
  does), gust, refH:10, alpha, aloftK, veerDeg, gradH, terrain, breeze, thermals: 0..1 }`.
- The frame is x east, z south (north = −z): a wind FROM bearing θ has base = spd·[−sin θ, 0, cos θ]
  (from north → toward +z ✓, from west → +x ✓; written `0 − sin` so a calm never carries a −0).
- rich = terrain / breeze / thermals > 0, or aloftK / veerDeg named. Not rich → `windLegacy`. No spec
  → the shared exact zero.

**The linearised sampler (K0, measured).** The field is two kinds of term. The SURFACE LAYER
k(agl) — the legacy power law — is steep curvature within metres of the ground, so it is NEVER
linearised: every call evaluates it exactly (one `pow`) from an AGL. Everything else has a scale
of 300 m or more and rides a Jacobian. `smooth()` writes seven channels at unit shear — the
synoptic column without k (base, then the speed and the veer toward the gradient wind above 300 m:
Ekman, aloftK 1.3 / veerDeg 20 declared), the terms that do not shear with the ground (K1 the
terrain flow, K3 the breeze and the thermals), and the turbulence intensity the gusts ride — and
the wind is `k·col + rest`, the gust amplitude `gust·k·TI`. A call at a NEW instant is the
reference: three terrainH (the ground and its gradient) and four `smooth()` (the channels and their
forward-difference Jacobian at 10 m). A call at the same instant within 40 m rides the Jacobian —
and UNDER 60 m AGL reads the ground exactly (the take-off roll and the final are flown on the true
ground, as the legacy field was; the analytic world's 65 m octave made a linearised AGL worth a
metre). A far call at the same instant (the Munk moment's `z = 0`, the pilot's `windAt`) is a full
sample and leaves the reference alone. Measured (GATE CLIMATE §7): over the solver's footprint
(±12 m, ±2 m) above 20 m AGL the linearised wind is within 0.06 m/s (0.55 %) of the full field; a
full sample is 0.8 µs; 25 calls per instant cost 4 full + 24 linear (ratio 6).

## 5. The physics (constants declared, justified, cuts named)

`w = U_syn ⊙ m_crest + w_terrain + w_breeze + w_thermal + w_downburst + gust·TI`

- **Synoptic column** (K0): 0–300 m AGL the legacy power law exactly; 300 m → gradH speed × aloftK,
  direction veered by veerDeg; constant above. Storm (K6): `U0·(1 + (windK − 1)·I)`, a veer through
  the passage, `gust·(1 + (gustK − 1)·I)`.
- **Terrain-following flow** (K1; linear hill theory, neutral): `w_y = terrain·[(U_h·∇h_c)·e^(−agl/1200)
  + (U_h·∇h_f)·e^(−agl/300)]`, the coarse band smoothed 1.2 km, the fine band 300 m, |∇h| ≤ 0.7. At the
  ground it is the kinematic condition (the air follows the slope): windward = lift, lee = sink.
- **Crest speed-up / valley shelter** (K1; Jackson–Hunt ΔS ≈ 1.6·h/L, cap 0.8): `m_crest = 1 +
  terrain·[S·prom⁺ − 0.4·S·prom⁻]·e^(−agl/300)`, floor 0.5, horizontal only.
- **Turbulence intensity** (K1): `TI = 1 + 3·smoothstep(0.25, 0.5, lee)·e^(−agl/600) + 0.5·(w*/2)[z < z_i]
  + (gustK − 1)·I` — rotors are separated flow; a declared amplitude model, not a derivation.
- **Sea / land breeze** (K3): `V = breeze·4 m/s·F·e^(−|d|/D)·∇d·(1 − z/700)⁺`, `F = max(0, sinEl(utc − 2 h))
  ·(1 − 0.8·cover) − 0.25·[night]`, D = 20 km inland / 8 km seaward; w_y from continuity. Cuts: the
  front's sharpness, the return flow aloft.
- **Thermals** (K3; Lenschow 1980 / Allen 2006): `w* = (g/T · H/(ρc_p) · z_i)^{1/3}`, `H = heat·(1 − alb)
  ·1361·0.75·sinEl_lag·(1 − 0.7·cover)`; `z_i = mixH` (= the LCL under `lapse:'mixed'` with cu/cb — the
  thermal top IS the cloud base). A square lattice of spacing 1.5·z_i in the frame advected at the BL
  wind (no per-thermal state); per cell a hash → exists (probability 0.3 + 0.6·coverage from the
  climate's own copy of the cloud weather map — thermals sit under the cumulus; the day owns
  `cloudSeed`), a jitter, a strength k ∈ [0.6, 1.4] × the raster's heat × the sun-facing aspect; life
  1200 s with a smooth envelope on the DAY clock. Profile `w̄(z) = w*·(z/z_i)^{1/3}·(1 − 1.1 z/z_i)`,
  `r₂(z) ≈ 0.102·(z/z_i)^{1/3}·(1 − 0.25 z/z_i)·z_i`; core `w = w_pk·(1 − (r/r₂)²)`, a sink annulus
  to 2r₂ at −w_pk/4 — mass-balanced exactly (gated). Tilt with the shear. Night → exact zeros.
- **Downbursts** (K6; cb, I > 0.5): the same lattice at 3 km / 600 s, `w = −6·I·(1 − z/1500)`, r₂ 800 m,
  the radial outflow from continuity (the gust front). Cut: cold-pool dynamics.
- **The atmosphere profile** (K2, 05_atmos): `makeAtmos` branches FIRST — no new field → the existing
  closed form untouched. With `lapse:'mixed' | mixH | inversion`: layers [mixed 0.0098 to mixH]
  [inversion −dT/thick] [ISA 0.0065 to 11 km] [isothermal], each integrated exactly; sigma-primary
  unchanged so sigma(0) ≡ 1. Humidity: Td(h) linear from the surface dewpoint to saturation exactly
  at cloudBase (under the mixed layer that IS the 1.8 K/km dew-point lapse: the 125 m/K rule and the
  profile agree); rh = 1 through a ≥ 0.5 deck. Dry density stays (< 1 % at 30 °C, declared).
- **The day extension** (K2, 07_day): `lapse`, `mixH`, `inversion{dT, thick}`, `diurnalC` (default 0 →
  no behaviour change), `storm{at, pre, dur, post, veerDeg, windK, gustK, dTemp, dQnh, cover, type}`,
  `seaTau` (0 = today's immediate rebuild), `cloudSeed`. Derived: the effective oatC / qnh, the storm's
  phase, cloudBase quantised to 20 m only while a swing or a storm moves it, `airKey`. The world's
  atmos getter rebuilds lazily on `airKey`.
- **The sea** (K4): `seaFrom` split into `draw(n)` (the seeded LCG — the same numbers) and
  `apply(A, L, dir, t₀)` with `φ' = φ + (ω' − ω)·t₀` so the temporal phase is continuous; the legacy
  setWind calls it with t₀ = 0 (bit-identical). With `seaTau > 0` and the viewer's tick, (A, L, dir)
  relax toward the smoothed surface wind — continuous everywhere in time; the far-field ridges slide
  at ~0.02 rad/s at 1 km (named, invisible at τ ≥ 120 s).
- **The clouds' drift** (K4): `cloudDrift(i, utc)` = the tabulated integral of `aloft(base_i)·1.5·driftK`
  over the day's timeline, pure in utc — a constant wind gives today's `w·secs` exactly; a front's
  veer moves the sky smoothly.

## 6. The relief raster (K0, lazy, node too)

200 m cells over the world's bounds (the analytic world 121², Jolene ≈ 156 × 196), centres at
`x0 + (i + 0.5)·200`. Twelve Float32 channels interleaved per cell: `h` (exact terrainH), `hc`
(a three-pass box blur of radius 6 cells ≈ a 6.5-cell Gaussian, ~1.2 km), `hf` (the radius-1 blur
minus hc: the fine band), their central-difference gradients, `prom` (hf / (rms₂ₖₘ(hf) + 5), ±1),
`coast` (a 3-4 chamfer distance, + inland, − at sea; the sea is level 0 AND classed water — the rule
waterH stands on, so a lake makes no breeze; ±30 km), its unit gradient (inland), and `heat` (0..1
from `surface()`: water 0, forest 0.25, grass 0.35, gravel/sand 0.5, paved/scree 0.55, rock 0.6;
refined by the island's terrain type when it has one — a Bowen-ratio ordering, declared). Built on
the first `setWind` that names terrain / breeze / thermals > 0 — never for a legacy spec. Measured:
52 ms on the analytic world; a bilinear read of all twelve channels ≈ 40 ns; `relief.hash` (FNV)
fingerprints it for the gate.

## 10. Risks, traps, owed

Two clocks (asserted §15); the shared WV (never allocate; `sample(out)` writes into `out`); the cloud
refit thrash (the 20 m base quantum, the 0.05 cover steps); the sampler budget (no wind texture on
the ground; the AP atlas rows are the only shared-texture precedent and this plan does not use them);
`aicSig` rebuilds under a richer field (measure); the pilot's `gustAt` will read thermal / lee
turbulence as gust and raise Vref (right; no calibrated preset turns a rich term on); the autopilot
samples the wind once at arrival (re-sample at FINAL only if K6's playtest shows it); the Munk
`z = 0` call (30_solver.js) is owed — the sampler treats it as a far call; bench/ is absent in the
worktrees (the analytic world's relief carries the ridge case); the CDP rigs are measurements, not
gates; CRLF; G-numbers at landing.

Owed after K6: lee / mountain waves (`waves` parsed, ignored); rotor dynamics; katabatic / anabatic
slope winds; the breeze front's sharpness and return flow; moist density; precipitation; the audio
vario; diegetic thermal markers (soaring birds, dust devils); the shed's windsock and sky under a
storm; shadow-map sway; a ridge tint on the ground; an island-world SOAR case; FOG-MIST F1 reading
`pub.haze.visibilityKm`; the 15 m motorglider "won't climb" debt; `gustAt`'s 61 × 4 samples at
departure (measure).
