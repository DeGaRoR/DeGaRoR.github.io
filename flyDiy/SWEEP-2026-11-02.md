- **Carriers**: a branch counts as SUPERSEDED when its unlanded commits or patches sit inside another branch, or when HANDOVER / reports name a successor.
- **Rulings**: every branch with unlanded src was checked against master's HANDOVER.md, BACKLOG.md and reports/*-READY.md for a word (landed / parked / held / shelved / cancelled / retired / reverted). Where the notes are silent, the branch is DROPPED (silent).
