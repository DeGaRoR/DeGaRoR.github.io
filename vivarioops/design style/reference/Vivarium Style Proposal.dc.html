3Procedural depth — generated maps, three-layer anatomy, gene-driven appendages

The elaboration comes from genes that were already in the schema and unspent — patternScale, stripeAnisotropy, patternContrast, iridescence. Each creature generates its own colour, roughness and bump maps at birth, wears three material layers, and grows fins, cilia and limb pairs by threshold. Still zero authored instances, still capsules and ellipsoids only per 10 §A10 — the elegance is in the profile curve and the layering, not in new primitives.

2cLuminosity as a trait — the rule, not the instance

Bioluminescence rides an existing gene, so it inherits, mutates and drifts without a single new field in the schema or one authored creature.

01Trigger: material.iridescence > 0.82 switches the body's emissive on. Uniform draw over [0,1] ⇒ about 1 in 6 creatures glows — the tank shows one most generations, never every generation.
02Inheritable: it is one scalar under the existing jitter operator, so a luminous parent usually yields luminous children and occasionally loses it. That is a lineage the player can pursue and fail at.
03Colour: emissive uses the creature's own ramp stop from 1g, never white — so a glowing creature is still legibly this world's animal.
04Phase-locked: brightness follows each segment's oscillator, so the glow is a readout of the gait — 1f's gate value, kept, but only on the creatures that earned it.
05Not a capability. Presentation only — nothing in L2 or L3 reads it. If it ever wants ecological meaning it needs its own measurement first.

One threshold constant in tokens or world config + one branch in render/creature.js. No schema change, no gate change.

Threshold is the only dial: 0.82 gives ~1 in 6, 0.9 gives ~1 in 10 and makes it a genuine event.

1Global UI styling + creature render direction — Vivarioops 0.7.1

The premise I am designing against: chrome is authored, creatures are not. So this proposal is two separate artifacts — a fixed chrome system (a diff to tokens.css + widgets.js, not a rewrite) and a presentation contract that constrains generated material without authoring any instance of it. P1, applied to art direction.

ASSUMPTION 01

N16 already forbids hex and raw px in components, so a restyle is a token swap. All three shells below are the same DOM as ui/base.css ships today.

ASSUMPTION 02

The render is currently candy: the hue gene walks the whole HSL wheel at 0.35–0.75 saturation, so six unrelated hues share one tank and it stops reading as one ecosystem. flatShading also reads as furniture — the thing 10 §A10 was trying to avoid.

ASSUMPTION 03

B3 and B4 are unsigned looking checkpoints. A render treatment that makes undulation legible is doing gate work, not decoration — 1f is aimed straight at "does it undulate or twitch".

1gPalette contract — how a generated hue becomes an authored colour

Authoring a ramp is authoring presentation, so P1 holds: no instance is authored, and the hue gene keeps every bit of its range. It just lands somewhere that belongs to the world.

01hue indexes the world's ramp instead of the HSL wheel. Six creatures now share a family; two worlds never look alike.
02hueVariance spreads across adjacent stops only — a recursive chain reads as one animal, never a harlequin.
03valueShift owns lightness; patternContrast owns saturation inside a bounded band. Nothing generated can reach full chroma.

05Rust and plum are the rare stops: two of six, so a warm creature is an event. Chrome never uses them — amber stays the stranger, and nothing else.
