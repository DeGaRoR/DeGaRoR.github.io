What is rigorous here?

Two eyes, two wheels. Each eye samples a finite-angle, distance-decaying light field. A 2×2 signed matrix maps sensor intensity directly to wheel velocity. Positive weights excite; negative weights inhibit. The chassis follows differential-drive kinematics. No agent knows positions, identities, goals, or the global scene.

The anthropomorphic names describe what an observer tends to perceive—not hidden emotions.
