Connections

Nodes sit on each unit edge — process units read top = in, bottom = out, a left/right node is the separated second output.

• Pick Conveyor or Vehicle, then tap the start node and the end node. The geometry routes itself.
• Conveyors draw as orthogonal elbows (right-angle belts with rounded corners).
• Vehicle routes find a path through the dirt roads and render as a smooth multi-point curve — they never cut across the building.
• Optional: tap a few points between the two nodes to steer the route (conveyor points stay in the warehouse, vehicle points stay on dirt).
• Crossings: the connection drawn last sits on top. Tap a line to select, then Delete.
• One finger pans, two fingers zoom. Switch to Units to nudge a unit — its links re-route automatically.
